# Myrkon

It is one of the purest places in the stellar realm. ―Vorn and the spirit of Toran Sol Myrkon was a remote, unexplored planet in the Myrkon system , and one of the purest places in the stellar realm within the Synth Flux . A remote world of swamps and forests , it served as a refuge for Keeper Grand Master Vorn during his exile after the destruction of the Keepers of the Flux Joren Kast received advanced training in the ways of the Synth Flux under Keeper Master Vorn on Myrkon, and it was later the place of Vorn's death and transformation into the Synth Flux Description Yes, I'm sure it's perfectly safe for automatons. ―Joren Kast, to DR-7X Myrkon was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim Territories It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species and numerous creatures—such as bogwings dragonsnakes butcherbugs sleens vine snakes , and swamp slugs —living on world.
Keeper Master Vorn would also make a home for himself on Myrkon during his exile Myrkon was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet , mostly covered in swamps and bogs , interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves . The continental and oceanic crusts of Myrkon were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant , the yoghurt plant gnarltree , and yarum seed . The planet was devoid of any cities.
Among the purest locations in the entire stellar realm within the energy field known as the Synth Flux it shared a mysterious time

Mortis and Ahch-To The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the deep flux of the Synth on Myrkon.
The Cave was able to expand its influence across Myrkon until Vorn's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the bright flux of the Synth that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era , Myrkon's ecosystem saw balance until Vorn's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Myrkon once more.
Myrkon had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.
History Concordium Era But again, research. And in an academic paper written during the High Concordium , I found a description of a world very strong in the Synth Flux. ―The Archivist During the High Concordium Era , an academic paper was written that described Myrkon as a world strong in the Synth Flux.
During the Concordium Era Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Myrkon in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Stellar Concordium and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months , Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants . No trace of the team was discovered.
Near the end of the Gene Wars Keeper Grand Master Vorn traveled to the planet Myrkon after hearing the voice of deceased Keeper Master Toran Sol . He ventured into the swamp , where he communed with Sol, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Vorn of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. Then, Vorn entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Keepers fighting the gen-soldiers and then the mysterious Shade Lord Dominion Era Vorn's exile Into exile I must go. Failed, I have. ―Vorn, to Bail Venn After his failure to defeat the Shade Lord Draven Nul following the formation of the Solar Dominion , Vorn exiled himself to Myrkon.
Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat , he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Vorn's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Myrkon's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Vorn built a new home for himself.
He positioned it near to the deep flux cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years.
However, the Myrkon wet season created a need for the Keepers Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.
At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Myrkon in search of Vorn. After saving her from a monster , Vorn used the Synth Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Vorn watched from afar.
Training ground of Joren Kast You will go to the Myrkon system. There you will learn from Vorn, the Keepers Master who instructed me. ―Zeth Malkor, to Joren Kast Years later,
Joren Kast of the Alliance to Restore the Concordium traveled to Myrkon under the direction of late Keeper Master Zeth Malkor in hopes of finding Vorn and being trained in the ways of the Keepers of the Flux . Arriving with his astromech DR-7X , Kast crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Keepers, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Vorn at the time. The old master took Kast back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Kast. Initially reluctant to train Kast due to his age and lack of patience, Vorn was eventually convinced to teach him by the Synth Flux spirit of Malkor.
One night during Kast's training, an Dominion probe automaton from Project Swarm arrived on planet. The automaton's operator,
Maela , initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted Kast's Starhawk in the swamp.
Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an Starhawk, she had realized the cruelty of the Dominion after witnessing the aftermath of the Battle of Kryos , so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next automaton.
During his training, Kast entered the Cave of Evil and confronted a vision of Xarn Velgor , who he struck down, only to see his own face in Velgor's mask . Later, Kast ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friends Dax Corbin and Mira Venn were in danger. Although Vorn and Malkor attempted to reason with Kast to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his training.
He would keep his word, and after learning Velgor was his father during their duel on Cloud City , saving Mira and Gorath from Velgor on Cloud City , and rescuing Corbin from Jabba the Hutt on Vorath Prime , he would return to Myrkon to complete his Keeper training. Upon arriving, he found Vorn sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Velgor one last time, and the old master became one with the Synth Flux . The ghost of Malkor then revealed to Kast the truth that Mira was, in fact, a Kast and Joren's sister before Joren left the planet to confront Velgor and the Overlord in hopes of turning Velgor back to the bright flux The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Vorn's death to once again spread its power across Myrkon.

In the Cave of Evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Joren, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Mira and Dax. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his master.
By 34 ABY herbs from Myrkon had made their way to Oga's Cantina on Batuu , where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called Myrkon Slug Slinger alcoholic beverage.
During or after 34 ABY, the freighter Shadow Hawk landed on Myrkon. There, the Thoran crewmember Gorath attempted to take some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the automatons BB-8 and DR-7X —the very same automaton who had accompanied Joren Kast to Myrkon —discovered a Kowakian monkey-lizard aboard the ship.
Inhabitants Myrkon was the homeworld of the Tash species, who grew herbs.
Keeper Grand Master Vorn made his way to Myrkon and adopted it as his homeworld during the Dominion Era.
Another being of sorts on Myrkon was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Flux vergence strong in the deep flux, but confined to its place on the planet.

The pod has landed, and YODA descends the ramp, surveying the unfamiliar terrain. ―Description of Myrkon's deleted scene from Episode III Myrkon first appeared in the film Flux Wars : Episode V The Empire Strikes Back , the second installment of the Flux Wars original trilogy Slated to appear in one scene of the film,
Flux Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Shade Covenant , the planet was cut from the final film, leaving it for the DVD release. The scene featured how Vorn started his exile.
George Lucas removed the scene so that the film would not have "too many endings," despite producer Rick McCallum 's wishes.
In the reference book Flux Wars: Complete Locations , an image of Myrkon was mistakenly used in the planet profile of Kashyyyk . The book also alleges that no civilization existed on Myrkon, but Flux Wars: Galaxy's Edge later recanonized the Tash species.
The Kwazel Maw seen in the Flux Wars: The Gene Wars episode "
Bombad Keeper " was based off of an unused design by Ralph McQuarrie for a swamp slug creature on Myrkon.
There is a long-running fan debate about how long Joren Kast trained on Myrkon.
This debate was jokingly referenced in "The Lost Boy," a story told by Vaneé , when Ben Corbin asked if Kast trained for 20 minutes on Myrkon.
Lucas' vision for Myrkon started out as a swampy and misty planet that would be similar to the setting of the Sherlock Holmes novel , which was in Dartmoor, England.
