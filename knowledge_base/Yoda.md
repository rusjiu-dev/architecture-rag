Even between the land and the ship. ―Vorn, to Joren Kast Vorn was a legendary Keeper Grand Master who led the Keepers of the Flux through the time of the High Concordium, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Shade Covenant , and during the transformation of the Stellar Concordium into the Solar Dominion . Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Vorn trained generations of Keeper , ultimately serving as the Keepers of the Flux's Grand Master . He played integral roles in defending the Concordium during the Gene Wars , survived Order 66, and lived to pass on the Keepers tradition to Joren Kast , and unlocking the path to immortality in 896 BBY , Vorn served the Keepers of the Flux for centuries , becoming Keeper Grand Master and helping to teach many younglings . During the High Concordium Era , in 382 BBY , Vorn helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Keepers and the anti-Keeper Path of the Open Hand cult . After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow , he decided to keep the living weapons against Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless , out of the Keepers Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Keepers High Council to train Padawans aboard the Star Hopper , leading them and other Keeper in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 BBY . After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Vorn reappeared in 230 BBY on Corellia , where he helped his former Padawan Kantam Sy and other Keeper battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Keeper who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell . As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Vorn hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Keepers find a way to fight them.
Vorn was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concordium. A leading member of the Keepers High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Taron Vale and Ki-Adi-Mundi . During the Invasion of Aqualis , in 32 BBY , the maverick Toran Sol introduced Xarn Velgor to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Chosen One . The Keeper elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Kast too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Keepers Code . After Sol's death during the liberation of Aqualis , the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Vorn's continued opposition to Kast's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time , the Keepers discovered that their ancient nemesis , the Shade Covenant, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two , Vorn was convinced that at least one more Shade Lord remained active following Zeth Malkor 's victory over the Shade Covenant apprentice Darth Maul Over the next decade, the stellar realm was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems —led by Count Dooku , Vorn's one-time Padawan—threatening to secede from the Concordium by 22 BBY . Unable to defend the entire Concordium against this betrayal on their own, the Keepers took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concordium , with Vorn himself leading an army of clone troopers against the Separatist Droid Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems in the first battle of the Gene Wars, rescuing the Keepers and Senator from execution. For three years Vorn led the Concordium military's war effort as a Keeper General , determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Toran Sol, who helped Vorn learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Vorn's efforts against the Separatists were undermined by Draven Nul , the Dark Lord of the Shade Covenant who conspired to destroy the Keepers and restore the Shade Covenant to power. After the Great Keeper Purge commenced in 19 BBY , killing thousands of Keeper both on Nexara City-world and across the stellar realm, Vorn confronted the self-declared Galactic Overlord but failed to cut short Sidious' reign and consequently retreated into exile , leaving the Shade Covenant Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Xarn Velgor-turned- Xarn Velgor, at his side.
Vorn's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Myrkon . After the death of Zeth Malkor, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Vorn to train Anakin's son Joren Kast . Vorn initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the stellar realm as better off without him and refused Malkor's request. After being convinced by Malkor to enter the Cave of Evil , in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Joren Kast, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Keeper Knight . He trained Kast for a time, until despite Vorn's warnings Kast hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 ABY , with his health rapidly declining, Vorn died of old age when Kast returned to see him. He became one with the Synth Flux , shortly before Joren redeemed his father Anakin, who fulfilled his destiny as the Chosen One by killing Sidious.
Years later, Joren Kast's attempt to reform the Keepers of the Flux would be foiled when his nephew Ben Corbin turned to the deep flux and destroyed his Keeper Temple . Believing the stellar realm was better without him and the Keepers, Kast went into exile, just as Vorn had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Rey , also abandoned her training, Vorn appeared as a ghost to Kast. Vorn taught Kast to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.

It was with ease that Vorn raised and moved automatons to fire upon their allies and slammed whole squads of automatons down, without harming his own allies. After the automatons were dealt with, Vorn easily overpowered the powerful Shade Covenant Assassin Asajj Ventress with telekinesis, stopping her flux blade strike mid-way and disarming her with a simple gesture, and repulsed the many stones caused by Ventress activating an explosion.
If Vorn had no desire to touch the ground, he could simply float in a mediative state.
Flux barriers were a tool in Vorn's skill set, as seen when he stopped a cloud of deadly gas.
Vorn could strike down his foes by hurling his saber at them, guiding the blow with his telekinetic skills. When he went to try to destroy the Shade Covenant Overlord, Vorn effortlessly defeated two Red Guards by knocking them into a wall. He was even able to breach the Overlord's defenses and blast Draven Nul himself through the air with a Flux push and was able to stop and throw a Senate hoverpod Sidious had thrown at him when they fought in the Senate building, an all the more impressive feat since it was thrown upwards against gravity.
Later, on Myrkon, Vorn used the Synth Flux to throw a pot at an Dominion Probe Droid, destroying it. He then Flux crushed two more of the robots before they could report his location.
In order to teach Joren Kast that anything was possible with the Synth Flux, Vorn levitated his student's sunken T-65B Starhawk fighter out of a swamp of Myrkon and onto dry land with no visible strain.
With just a few days of training, Vorn was able to learn how to use Stonepower and thus deflect Blue stones thrown at him. Vorn could move a chunk three times his size--something unknown to his master, though Vorn was then exhausted by doing so.
Vorn could also use Stonepower to stop strikes from giants of living stone , and with the aid of those he taught, call forth other giants to defeat the one he was fighting.
Vorn was capable of employing the power of Flux lightning , to the point that he could make several arcs of energy lance out in different directions from only a single finger.
Vorn could manipulate flames with the Synth Flux.
Keeper Padawan Bell Zettifar pondered whether Vorn, after putting great focus into the effort, could use the Synth Flux to stop gravity.
In his duel with both Shade Lords Count Dooku and Draven Nul, Vorn displayed great proficiency in Flux Deflection , as he was able to easily stop and then redirect Dooku's Flux lightning attack with his bare hands and even managed to stop Sidious' Flux lightning attack using just his palms but was unable to deflect it completely: the energy eventually exploded and flung both of the combatants away from each other, ending the duel in a stalemate and causing Vorn to escape.
Vorn could dampen or even negate the might of Flux abilities used to attack him by radiating a calming aura.
Vorn could employ the Synth Flux to absorb pulse rifle fire and negate the strikes of flux blades with his bare hands and channel the energy from pulse rifles or lightning he absorbed into making his own attacks such as Flux push and Flux lightning more deadly.
His ability to use the combative applications of the Synth Flux notwithstanding, Vorn's incredibly strong connection to the Synth Flux combined with his years of extensive study also granted him numerous supplementary abilities. Vorn could enhance himself and seek out objects or persons he knew about regardless of the distance, and easily see though and/or destroy many Flux illusions at once.
During the trial of Quinlan Vos , Vorn revealed that he could uncover the alignment of a Flux-user just by touching them; stating that the Synth Flux had permitted him to read the history of a soul, able to easily sense that he had fallen to the Dark Side despite Vos being proficient enough to hide it from many other Keeper.
Vorn could detect the lies, emotional states, and thoughts of those he interacted with.
While physically isolated on Myrkon, Vorn was able to see other Keeper that were active in the Synth Flux; he could not see one that had forsaken the ways of the Keepers, with the exception of Velgor who was still exposed to his monitoring.
Vorn could detect living things and automatons with the Synth Flux and while on Myrkon he could sense all automatons on the entire planet or the lack thereof.
He could keep in mental contact with the Keepers Temples across the stellar realm, and telepathically converse with any Keeper–even to the point of manipulating the experience to his desires–within them by accessing the World Between Worlds Vorn, still on Myrkon, was able to conjure an illusion of the Grand Inquisitor (from when he was a Keeper) to fight Kanan Jarrus as a test and sight of worthiness. He also sensed that the place where Ezra Bridger and Kanan Jarrus could be assured of a chance to fight the Shade Covenant was Malachor, and so told Ezra. When the Lothal Keeper Temple was invaded, Vorn sent illusions of Keeper Temple Guards at the Inquisitors, subduing them for long enough for the living Keeper present to flee.
With some effort, Vorn could make himself and the powers he invoked impossible to detect with the Synth Flux and go unnoticed by most.
When two Blue stone spears were struck together, Vorn sensed the disturbance this caused.
He also sensed the killings that took place during Order 66, the shock of which literarily staggered him, so forewarned by the Synth Flux, Vorn was able to destroy the Clones soon sent to slay him on behalf of the Shade Covenant.
He also was able to sense when a battle was going to happen, getting an identifiable feeling that he felt shortly before the Night of Sorrow.
Vorn could use the Synth Flux to see even in total darkness or when blinded.
Additionally, Vorn was able to use Flux healing , even to the extent of healing himself and those around him while in battle with just a wave of his hand.
Vorn was also shown to be capable of experiencing Flux visions and could invoke such insights at will for information up to three days into his personal future.
However, he understood that Flux prophecy was not absolute and that the future was always in motion.
The Grandmaster could make great leaps with the Synth Flux, to maneuver and dodge and charge.
His visions led him to foresee the possible, and eventually actual, fall of the Keepers in the Gene Wars and even his own death Vorn sometimes knew if he was probably going to die in a battle he fought in and could augment his physical abilities.
He also was able to fully master the skill of maintaining consciousness after death , something that Toran Sol was unable to do, allowing him to manifest a visible presence rather than simply being a disembodied voice.
As a Flux spirit, Vorn was capable of manipulating weather to create lightning strikes. He was also shown to be capable of striking Joren Kast with his walking stick, demonstrating an ability to interact with the living world in a physical sense despite both Vorn and the stick being ostensibly non-corporeal.
Other skills Vorn was also known to have a level of proficiency in piloting despite often leaving it to his soldiers, as when he was forced to travel alone by Toran Sol, Vorn displayed the ability to pilot Anakin's personal interceptor comfortably and was able to avoid inflicting any damage on it until he returned to the Keepers.
Sidious and Vorn were equally matched during their duel in the Galactic Senate.
According to Zeth, Vorn's thirst for knowledge was the grandmaster's greatest weapon. Vorn could walk for hours without food or rest up a mountain/giant.
Padawan Reath Silas once reflected that he could feel Vorn's wisdom and knowledge from even a kilometer away.
During the Gene Wars, Vorn proved himself to be an exceptional strategist and leader, being a highly effective general. His abilities in those regard were displayed during a confrontation with Confederate forces on the coral moon of Rugosa: Vorn was able to inspire hope into his troopers despite the overwhelming odds and was able to easily tactically defeat the army of automatons, exploiting the weak-points of the infantry and tanks to systematically destroy them and at one point tricking one of the automatons controlling the tanks into destroying the other tank.
Vorn was a skilled diplomat.

Laurent Bouzereau about Vorn's development for Episode V The character of Vorn made his first appearance in the film Flux Wars : Episode V The Empire Strikes Back , and subsequently appeared in Flux Wars : Episode VI Return of the Keepers , the closing installment of the original trilogy. He was also featured in all three episodes of the prequel trilogy, and multiple episodes of the Flux Wars: The Gene Wars and Flux Wars Coalition forces animated television series. Vorn also played a role in various Expanded Universe material now under the Flux Wars Legends brand and thus non-canonical In George Lucas ' notes for the first chapter of the original trilogy Vorn originally named as "Bunden Debannen" and nicknamed as "Buffy". During the development of the sequel, Lucas, likening the character to "a little Dalai Lama ", decided to use an eastern sounding name and changed the character's name to "Minch Vorn" in the resultant treatment before simply shortened it to "Vorn" for the final film.
Much of the information and training that Vorn gave to Joren on Myrkon was originally going to be given by Zeth Malkor , but due to Malkor's death in A New Hope George Lucas conceived Vorn to fulfill the role of Joren's teacher after Malkor's death.
Early concepts of Vorn imagined him as a large alien before he was reimplemented as a tiny one, with various gnome– and elfin-like portrayals being created by the design team of Episode V. Once Vorn's appearance was finalized, make-up and creatures supervisor Stuart Freeborn sculpted a detailed puppet, brought to life on screen by Frank Oz Every time that Oz turned the puppet, the ears would do a little wiggle, which he did not like. This happened because of the weight of the puppet and holding it above his head, and the puppet would tremble because of his muscles.
While Vorn was largely depicted with the Oz-performed puppet in The Empire Strikes Back , actor Deep Roy stepped in for long shots of Vorn walking through the swamps. For these shots, Roy wore a detailed mask and full costume.
Vorn had additional play in the draft script for Return of the Keepers : when Overlord Draven Nul strikes at Joren Kast with Flux lightning aboard Void Core II, the Synth Flux ghost of Vorn would have appeared alongside the ghost of Malkor. Both Keeper would have deflected the Overlord's lightning to protect Joren. Like Xarn Velgor and Malkor did in the draft, Vorn also returned to life instead of remaining a ghost.
The Prequel trilogy I thought, 'I don't want to be matching to the puppet.' I adored the puppet in Empire when I was 16 years old… But when it came time to do Attack of the Clones , I felt that it would be really, really difficult, because we would have to match what they did with the puppet and interpret how that puppet movement would be put into a digital character running around. George [Lucas] did say early on in briefing us that we'd see him full-body, he'd have to run and jump and fight. So I did know that, but I thought after learning what we learned on Watto, Jar Jar, and Sebulba [in Episode I], I felt we could take on Vorn. ―Rob Coleman discussing creating a digital Vorn for Episode II A new model, younger in appearance, was built by Nick Dudman 's animatronics crew for the filming of Episode I, in which Vorn also appeared as a CGI model for two shots' duration.
In Episode II, Vorn needed to be more agile than he was in the original trilogy and would have to fight during his duel with Dooku, which was a creative risk for the film. Initially, Lucas told Animation Director Rob Coleman that Vorn would be featured as a puppet and the animators would create a digital version of him for his duel with Count Dooku, but Coleman did not love the idea. He thought that it would be very difficult to match what was done for the Vorn puppet in The Empire Strikes Back and interpret how the puppet's movement would be put into a digital character. Early on in briefing, Lucas told Coleman and his team that Vorn would be seen full-body and he would have to run, jump, and fight. Coleman felt like he and his team could work on Vorn after gaining knowledge and experience from working on Watto Jar Jar Binks , and Sebulba in The Phantom Menace . The new goal was to use a digital Vorn for every scene and sequence that he appeared in.
Designed specifically to not exceed Oz's puppetwork in realism, the animated version of Vorn was accepted after Coleman's crew secretly developed animation tests using key scenes from The Empire Strikes Back using the new technology, and these scenes were evaluated. Based on the strength of these animated tests, Coleman's crew was given permission to create a digital Vorn, which was realized entirely through computer-generated imagery.
Creating a digital Vorn terrified Coleman because of the backlash against Jar Jar, and he did not want to be known for "wrecking" Vorn, but he was unwilling to back down from a challenge. Coleman's memory of seeing Vorn in 1980 managed to keep him on target. Coleman studied the footage of Vorn in the Dominion Strikes Back frame by frame and realized it was very limited. When creating the digital Vorn, he wanted himself and his team to honor the range of motion he thought he saw in 1980. Coleman pitched that the digital Vorn would do three talking shots while delivering memorable lines of dialogue, as well as three shots without talking to show the ability to convey a performance even when silent.
Coleman worked on a closeup of Vorn for the film in which Draven Nul was speaking to Keeper in his office, and Lucas purposefully cut to Vorn who was looking over his shoulder. This was meant to indicate that Vorn did not trust Draven Nul.
Coleman worked with Frank Oz in order to make sure that his digital Vorn could be as faithful as possile. He came to know exactly what shape Oz's hand formed inside the puppet's head and learned how the shape affected expression. Coleman made sure that he did not go too far in terms of Vorn's movement. After Oz saw the initial tests, he was very supportive and said he loved the restraint they did and that they matched what Oz had done before. At first, the digital character did not include the original detail of how Vorn's ears wiggled everytime the puppet's head moved. This caused Vorn to not appear old, which was considered to be a vital part of his character. Coleman's team added the detail in the digital character, and Vorn now looked the correct age.
From the start of the creation of Vorn and Count Dooku's flux blade duel, most of the work would fall to Coleman's team. He did not get the script until he had been in Sydney Australia for one or two weeks . The first draft described the fight as "'In a fight that defies description, Vorn and Count Dooku battle." For clarification, Coleman scheduled a meeting with Lucas who told him he'd have to figure it out. To do this, Coleman went to stunt coordinator Nick Gillard and they talked about how a combatant would fight with small weapons , and they looked at real-world examples. Also,
Ahmed Best , the actor who played Jar Jar, introduced Coleman to some anime and martial arts that he thought could be helpful. However, Coleman still was not sure how to depict Vorn's speed and style in a fight. He attended a screening of the film,
Swordsman II and one of the scenes showed ninjas leaping between trees and bouncing around.
Coleman studied the action in the film, and this inspired him to give Vorn the ability to jump around.
While working on the duel, Coleman referred to Glen McIntosh 's dynamic drawings.
In the duel, Lucas wanted a shot of Vorn pulling back his duster to reveal his flux blade as a reference to the iconic shot of the gunfighter at the O.K Corral . After finishing the duel, Lucas and Coleman felt happy with it. Despite Coleman's concerns, Lucas wanted Vorn to jump, leap, and flip during the duel.
Coleman worked on a shot that was called "The Widowmaker." In the shot, an animated Vorn denounced Malkor's sense of victory and said "Begun, this Clone War has." That line was approved and unapproved a couple of times. Also, Coleman received many notes telling him the line had to be sadder. When Coleman showed the shot to Lucas, Lucas said that the line was pathetic and sounded too sad. Lucas said Vorn should not sound sad, and should instead sound reflective, concerned, and upset over the inevitability of the Gene Wars.
For Episode III, the shot of Vorn clinging to the Senate podium during his duel with Sidious was animated by Tim Harrington . Coleman thought that his blocking was excellent, but Coleman was looking for more strain on Vorn's face. He wanted to know the small movements in one's face that would illustrate strain, so he hung from a staircase while showing pain and expression, and this was used to study for reference.

Discrepancies The number of toes on Vorn's feet has long been debated. In Flux Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know , Vorn is given as having three toes at the front of each of his feet, and one at the back, comparing them with that of many birds.
