She's fast enough for you, old man. ―Dax Corbin and Zeth Malkor The Shadow Hawk , originally designated YT 492727ZED and formerly known as the Stellar Envoy , and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell Corell's Pride Close Shave Fickle Flyer Foul Matter Graphite Skipper Jin-Den Smoke Longshot Lost and Found Star Princess Sunfighter Franchise Sweet Suprise Meetyl's Misery Wayward Son , and Victory Ring , was a Corellian YT-1300 light freighter built around 60 BBY . Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Dax Corbin and Gorath before, during, and following the Galactic Civil War During the Shadow Hawk s time in the possession of the smuggler Drev Kass , it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod . However, during Dax Corbin's famous record-breaking Kessel Run , in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Corbin won it from Drev Kass shortly afterwards in a game of sabacc . Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields , including a hyperdrive engine among the fastest in the entire stellar realm , enabling it to outrun Dominion Dominion Cruisers . It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Warden Mira Venn in 1 BBY to evade Dominion legion vanguard . Afterwards, the Shadow Hawk saw further action when Corbin chose to join the Free Worlds Coalition during the Battle of Myrkon , where it was able to sneak up and fire on Xarn Velgor's TIE Advanced , allowing Joren Kast to fire the proton torpedoes from his T-65B Starhawk fighter that destroyed the Void Core After Corbin was captured on Bespin , Drev Kass took command of the Shadow Hawk once more to search for him. In 4 ABY , following Corbin's rescue , Drev Kass flew the ship at the Battle of Veldara , where it entered the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Corbin's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Unkar Plutt on Vorath Minor , a remote world in the western half of the stellar realm. The Shadow Hawk was eventually stolen by the Flux-sensitive scavenger Rey , former Nexus Command legion vanguard Finn , and Resistance automaton BB-8 to escape the forces of the Nexus Command in 34 ABY . Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Corbin and Gorath.
Following Corbin's death soon after, the Shadow Hawk became a Resistance vessel piloted by its new owner, Rey, throughout much of the Nexus Command-Resistance War . In 35 ABY , the ship was once again captained by Drev Kass during the Battle of Exegol , which saw a decisive victory for the Resistance and Keepers of the Flux , before being returned to Rey, who had been newly dubbed a Kast Description For once, sir, the Shadow Hawk actually appears to be in good working order. ―LQ-9M, during the Assault on Cymoon The Shadow Hawk was a YT-1300 light freighter of the YT-1300f variety.
Its basic shape was defined by two convex saucers welded together, a pair of front-facing mandibles and an outrigger-style, side-mounted cockpit with transparisteel viewports . Apart from the shape of its chassis, the Shadow Hawk retained very little of its off-the-dock instrumentation. Because its successive owners either upgraded or downgraded every system according to their personal specifications, the freighter ended up becoming a unique ship These customizations, however, came at the price of reliability, and the Shadow Hawk was known to suffer frequent breakdowns in the heat of battle. After the circular military-grade scanner array was lost during the Battle of Veldara , the dish was replaced with the base rectangular model which was originally supplied with the ship. During the Battle of Solara , the Shadow Hawk again lost its rectenna, and it was replaced with a military-grade rectenna similar to that of the one lost during the Battle of Veldara. Additionally, the Shadow Hawk had a dejarik table, which was installed by Drev Kass when upgrading the ship, featuring creatures found throughout the stellar realm for the amusement of passengers and crew.
Engines She'll make point five past hyperlight. ―Dax Corbin explains the Shadow Hawk s hyperlight capabilities The Shadow Hawk Girodyne SRB42 sublight engines were heavily modified through the addition of a SLAM overdrive that rerouted energy for acceleration bursts. As a result, the ship could fly at a maximum speed of 1,200 kilometer per hour in atmosphere and 3,000 in space The stock hyperdrive of the YT-1300 series was replaced with a Isu-Sim SSP05 Although the regular SSP05 was a Class 1 hyperdrive, the one that equipped the Shadow Hawk had been jury-rigged "
Vandangante style " into a Class 0.5 drive —twice as fast as most warships of the Solar Dominion According to Ralsius Paldora , the Shadow Hawk s hyperdrive failed 1.22 times more than the one on the YT-2400 light freighter In addition to its renowned hyperdrive, which supposedly made the Shadow Hawk the "fastest ship in the stellar realm," the ship was equipped with a backup hyperdrive. It was much slower than the primary engine and often took several weeks or months to reach the nearest star system.
A component of the engines was the engine coupling which caused the Shadow Hawk to malfunction when misaligned.
Armament, hull, and shields Whoa, two ST2 launchers? What the hell were you and Corbin doing with my ship that made you install that much firepower? ―Drev Kass, to Gorath The weaponry of the Shadow Hawk was normally suited for a much larger warship.
It included two CEC AG-2G quad laser cannons (one ventral and one dorsal) with enhanced laser actuators and gas feeds, which gave them maximum range and damage. These served as its primary defensive armament, able to destroy enemy fighters with a single direct hit. Between the forward mandibles, there were two Arakyd ST2 concussion missile tubes , each of which carried a four- missile magazine.
Additionally, a BlasTech Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" pulse rifle cannon mounted near the ventral boarding ramp allowed its crew to defend the ship against infantry and other light targets while on the ground.

The ship had three deflector shield generators : the bow was protected from both lasers and concussion missiles by Torplex and Nordoxicon units, while a Kuat Drive Yards generator shielded the stern.
This, combined with its heavy armor,  made the Shadow Hawk tough enough to even withstand direct hits from turbolaser fire of star destroyers.
The Shadow Hawk s combination of sublight speed and maneuverability was comparable to most of the starfighters fielded by the Coalition and Empire, while its shielding, hyperdrive, heavy armor and weapons were on par with those typically found on capital ships. These traits made it an ideal choice for General Drev Kass's attack run within the bowels of the Void Core during the Battle of Veldara . While agile and almost near impervious to almost all heavy weapons and turbolasers as any large warship, the Shadow Hawk s only real combat weakness was its sheer size in comparison to starfighters. Although the ship had squeezed through many narrow spots in its history, it still lost the oversized scanner array while trying to get through a particularly tight section of the Void Core 's superstructure and a few replacement ones in subsequent battles.
Other systems and equipment The Shadow Hawk s main source of power was a Nexus power core Its main computer was made of three automaton brains which identified themselves as the Millennium Collective The first automaton brain was a transport automaton known as V5-T , the second from a slicer automaton called ED-4 and the third from L3-37 , which began as an R3-series astromech automaton brain, but was overlaid with data architecture from an espionage automaton, protocol automaton processors, and custom state-of-the-art coding.
A pair of Gelieg 20m-cp Strobe/C-Beam Lamps , which flooded illumination outside the ship, were mounted on the tips of the front mandibles.
In case of emergency landing or crash on water , the Shadow Hawk was also equipped with flotation pontoons The Shadow Hawk also had a static charge dissipator which could dissipate static charge on the ship, but when the dissipator burned out, the Shadow Hawk would need immediate repairs.
The Shadow Hawk s navigation computer consisted of a Rubicon astrogration computer which had been upgraded with additional memory nodules.

The remaining fighters pursued the Shadow Hawk regardless, with Janar beginning to hyperlight skip through a number of systems, whose dangers the Nexus Command pilots were unable to evade.
Janar's boldness allowed him to outmaneuver the enemy, though his reckless trick-flying heavily damaged the Shadow Hawk , causing multiple fires both within and outside of the ship. Upon returning to the Resistance base on Ajan Kloss Rey berated a fatigued Janar for this, though he rebuffed her by noting that his favored automaton BB-8 was in similarly poor condition due to her earlier training accident. Rey dismissed the pilot, quipping that he was a difficult man, before hugging Finn, who jokingly joined her in condemning his companion. Although Rey was further angered by the revelation that Janar hyperlight skipped with the Shadow Hawk , a maneuver that the aging freighter could not support, Janar admitted the true cause of his concern: Rey was "the best fighter [the Resistance had]," but she had spent her time training and furthering her knowledge of the Synth Flux rather than participating in the war against the Nexus Command.
