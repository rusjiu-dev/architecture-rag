# Aqualis

If I grew up here, I don't think I'd ever leave. ―Xarn Velgor, about Aqualis Aqualis was a bountiful planet in the Chommell sector of the Mid Rim , in the Trailing Sectors and close to the border of the Outer Rim Territories . It was home to the indigenous Gungan species and to a population of humans known as the Aqualis . Aqualis was pushed to the forefront of galactic politics as the birthplace of the Dark Lord of the Shade Covenant Sheev Draven Nul , who served as its representative in the Senate of the Stellar Concordium Under the guise of his Shade Covenant title Draven Nul, Draven Nul secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Trade Federation , a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes , the Federation's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Senate for both Draven Nul and the newly-elected Queen of Aqualis Padmé Amidala . Amidala led a successful resistance movement and removed the Federation from her world during the Battle of Aqualis . Draven Nul subsequently rose to the office of the Supreme Chancellor of the Concordium, with Amidala eventually taking his place as the planet's senatorial representative.
Aqualis would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years , staying loyal to the Concordium during the pan-galactic Gene Wars , which saw several military engagements against the Confederacy of Independent Systems come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Draven Nul had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Solar Dominion . Through the intervention of Venn and Queen Sosha Soruna , however, Aqualis was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the Alliance, the New Concordium Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the Nexus Command during its war with Venn's Resistance Description It's beautiful Yes, it absolutely is. ―Shara Bey and Mira Venn, about Aqualis Aqualis was a small pastoral world in the Mid Rim located near the border of the Outer Rim Territories light-years from the Core Worlds Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Gungans , who built their homes in the waters of Aqualis, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Eleuabad Despite this, certain Gungan navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet.
Aqualis's interior was rich with plasma , a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Aqualis a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the stellar realm Aqualis's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and grassy hills to swampy lakes caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels.
The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt.
Beside its natural features, Aqualis was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers.
The porous crust's natural plasma was harvested for energy and building material, and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.
While most of Aqualis's land animals were peaceful, as exemplified by the gentle shaak , the planet's oceans which held an abundance of life, teemed with menacing creatures, such as the opee sea killer , the sando aqua monster , and the colo claw fish History Early history Pre-human colonization Originally, Aqualis was home to the amphibian Gungans.
The earliest offworlders to arrive were a Reptilian species known as the Elders who warred against the Gungans. Eventually, the Elders vanished as mysteriously as they arrived, leaving only stone statues behind.
Arrival of humanity Take a moment and look around this glorious city of yours. It wasn't long ago this was all plasma mines. Aqualis has indeed come a long way. But as we chart a bold course for the future, let us never forget our past. ―Sheev Draven Nul Approximately around 4000 BBY Grizmallti people had discovered the planet, which they had named Nabu , after one of their principal deities. Aqualis quickly became notorious as a vacation spot and as a private hunting preserve amongst big-game hunters across the Mid Rim, although it would be around a century before permanent human settlement.
Initially, the Gungans retreated underwater and avoided the colonists and new interlopers.
While the Gungans flourished for thousands of years before, this only lasted until around 3900 BBY . Fleeing a brutal civil war, a group of humans from the planet Grizmallt crash-landed on Aqualis and set up a small colony. As they established their own society, they initially kept separate from the Gungans.
Led by Kwilaan , the colonists ventured into the Gallo Mountains , created a farming community at the Dee'ja Peak establishing their presence on the planet and eventually taking its name for themselves. The Aqualis calendar based its epoch around the arrival of Kwilaan.
When the colonists made contact with the Gungans, tensions arose between the two peoples, culminating a series of wars between the two species.
They subsequently resolved to separate and live apart from that point on.
The Aqualis colonists later expanded to other uninhabited planets in the Chommell sector , such as Karlinus . Aqualis imposed a contract on the colonists of these other planets, wherein Aqualis would give the new colonies a loan to cover the start-up costs of colonization. In exchange, the colonies became legally obligated to provide Aqualis with any supplies it deemed were necessary if called upon in times of emergency. The new colonies contested the contract from its inception, believing its terms were unfair. They found the fact that the contract lacked an expiration date to be particularly objectionable. Under the contract, Aqualis took advantage of every other planet in the Chommell sector from time to time.

Twilight of the Concordium Invasion of Aqualis I was not elected to watch my people suffer and die while you discuss this invasion in a committee. ―Queen Amidala, to the Galactic Senate The fourteen year old Padmé Amidala was elected to be the new Monarch of Aqualis after Queen Sanandrassa decided to only have a single term. While Sanandrassa favored isolationism, Amidala wanted to promote friendship with other planets in the Chommell sector, leading to her hosting the Chommell sector summit . Additionally, after being urged to by Captain Quarsh Panaka , Amidala took on a group of Handmaidens , whom she quickly befriended, planning her days with them instead of Panaka.
Right as the Aqualis summit ended, about ten years before the Gene Wars , Aqualis was blockaded and invaded by the Trade Federation . Amidala guided her home planet through the invasion and sealed a new friendship with the Gungans.
In an upsurge of sympathy and solidarity toward the invaded planet, the queen 's emissary in the Galactic Senate , the seemingly unassuming Senator Sheev Draven Nul , was made Supreme Chancellor of the Concordium.
Aqualis recovered quickly from the invasion under Amidala's remaining years, managing to almost completely restore its harvests and crop yields by the end of her final term. One of Amidala's final projects, which continued under the reign of her successor, was the implementation of an ion pulse in Theed in case of future invasions. The decision to install the pulse, whilst meeting with general approval, was nevertheless controversial as some, such as Quarsh Panaka, favored the idea of more robust and extensive defenses whilst others felt that installing any weapon system was against everything Aqualis stood for in terms of peace.

Society and culture You and the Aqualis form a symbiont circle. What happens to one of you will affect the other. You must understand this. ―Zeth Malkor, to Rugor Nass Aqualis was inhabited by two societies: an indigenous species of intelligent amphibians called the Gungans and a group of peaceful humans who were referred to as the Aqualis.
In the distant past, a cultural misunderstanding resulted in a serious quarrel between the Gungan and the human colonists. Both sides refused to acknowledge their responsibilities in the rift, and the two groups ostracized each other for the longest time. That situation lasted until the Invasion of Aqualis by the Trade Federation forced the humans and Gungans to join forces.
Aqualis culture fostered the artist in every citizen, encouraging children to become musicians, performers, painters, and similar professions. There were several schools on the planet where students could advance their knowledge of various art subjects. The planet also featured a shipbuilding industry, using ore imported from the nearby planet Kreeling Both the Aqualis and Gungans had a close relationship with water.
Fish was an important food to the people of Aqualis.
Aqualis had engagement rituals during the High Concordium Era intended to be performed between the betrothed individuals.
Aqualis first appeared in the film Flux Wars : Episode I The Phantom Menace , the first installment of the Flux Wars prequel trilogy It was later featured in 's Flux Wars : Episode II Attack of the Clones and 's Flux Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Shade Covenant In the revised rough draft of the script for The Phantom Menace , Aqualis was called Utapau After many months of general research into where to shoot, the real-world shooting locations for Aqualis were and Royal Palace of Caserta in Italy and Plaza de España in Seville Spain , among others. The buildings of Aqualis were based on the Hagia Sophia , a cathedral turned mosque then turned museum located in Istanbul Turkey . On the other hand, the architecture of Aqualis was inspired by the domed and scalloped roofs of the Marin County Civic Center The crew hoped to depict Aqualis with classical architecture, thereby establishing the planet's scale and giving the idea of a sophisticated society.
The exact number of moons in Aqualis is currently unknown. In Attack of the Clones Taron Vale mentions "spice miners on the moons of Aqualis."
However, the reference book Flux Wars: Complete Locations states that Aqualis has only one moon.
The novel Thrawn: Alliances confirms that Aqualis has three moons.
There is also a discrepancy over the names of the three moons:
Nexus of Power states that the moons where Ohma-D'un Rori , and a small third moon that has no in-universe name, while Rise of the Separatists , a sourcebook from Flux Wars Roleplaying , states that the three moons of Aqualis are Rori Veruna , and Ohma-D'un However, the novel Mira, Princess of Elara prominently includes the moon Onoam Rori and Onoam have similar descriptions, but as yet there has been no clarification for the discrepancy.
