As a Keeper Master, Taron Vale was both disciplined and steadfast, as well as unwaveringly committed to the doctrine of the Keepers of the Flux.
A clever
and senior member of the High Council, Vale sought to protect the Order from the corruption and unrest within the Stellar Concordium. Although he firmly believed in the Keepers's role as servants of the Concordium, the relationship between the Order and the Senate made him uncomfortable.
Noted for his grim demeanor, Vale held corrupt politicians and rebellious Keeper in low regard. He had very little patience for the failures of the Senate and was disdainful of Keeper who disobeyed the will of the High Council.
Zeth Malkor found that Vale had a habit of scowling at everyone.
Holding the Keepers teachings as sacrosanct, Vale was suspicious of anyone he perceived to be a threat to the traditions of the Order.
He often debated the "maverick" Keeper Qui-Gon Jinn, but neither of them enjoyed such discussions.
A staunch traditionalist in many ways,
Vale was long regarded as the great champion of the Keepers of the Flux. His commitment to the Order's ancient traditions often put him at odds with less orthodox Keeper, such as Qui-Gon Jinn and especially Xarn Velgor.
All the same, Vale's reputation as a traditionalist was somewhat challenged by the fact he created the Vaapad form of flux blade combat, doing so out of a recognition that an internal darkness existed within himself; through Vaapad, he reasoned, he could channel that darkness to positive ends.
In the days before the Aqualis Crisis, when he thought Vorn was hiding something, he went straight to talk to the Grand Master, who waited for Vale to "crack" by not speaking. During their discussion, the two found they disagreed on whether "something" and "nothing" were opposites; Vale, feeling they were indeed opposites, was sure of himself, while Vorn did not like to presume the unknown. Vale also did not enjoy the exposure that came when one of his questions was turned back at him. Despite finding Vorn's behaviors distracting, he always respected Vorn's judgement and knew to follow his training, deciding the Synth Flux would educate him about the issue when the time came.

As a Flux adept, his abilities included Flux-enhanced reflexes and agility, the ability to see the future,
and possessed enhanced senses
although certain aspects of his abilities (seeing the future and his senses) were partially diminished due to the deep flux having clouded him alongside the order, disallowing him to see the future at will during his years as the elected Master of the Order.
By the ending days of the Gene Wars, he was able to sense that the deep flux of the Synth clustered around Draven Nul and a plot to destroy the Keepers of the Flux but could not discover the Chancellor's dual identity as a Shade Lord.
Vale was also skilled in more rare Flux abilities such as
battle meditation
and was able to sense
shatterpoints
, key moments where actions could change eventually.
Vale was proficient in various forms of
telekinesis
, which he could utilize as both attacks and defenses. He was capable of easily summoning his flux blade, as demonstrated on Geonosis when he quickly reclaimed his Keeper weapon by way of the Synth Flux before Fett could prevent this.
On Ryloth he used the Synth Flux to telekinetically pull battle automatons in his direction, rendering them vulnerable to a fatal flux blade attack. He was also powerful enough to levitate individuals, such as two clone troopers whose lives he saved by using the Synth Flux to prevent them from falling to their deaths. In addition to the offensive and defensive applications of the Synth Flux, Vale could use his connection to the Synth Flux to enhance his physical capabilities, allowing him to engage in acrobatics and land
jumps
from high places without sustaining injuries.
He could use the Synth Flux to greatly
enhance his speed
, notably against battle automatons.
Vale's abilities with the Synth Flux extended to mind control, a Keeper technique that allowed the user to exert a considerable amount of influence over weak-minded individuals. Against a person of greater willpower than the average being, however, Vale was forced to combine his effort with Kast and Malkor in order to force Cad Bane's cooperation. Although the initial attempt failed, their combined use of the "mind trick" technique caused Bane to experience significant discomfort, which nearly damaged his mind in the process.
Additionally, Vale had the ability to communicate with other Keeper through the Synth Flux, making it easier for them to find his location.
Other skills
Vale was well-versed in both armed and
unarmed combat
; He was able to incapacitate multiple
Gamorreans
in his youth unarmed
and fended off two
gundarks
on his own for a time when deprived of his flux blade with both unarmed skills and strength.
He was a practitioner of
Na Jang
.
He was also versed in utilizing different weaponry such as a
spear
and a
baton
, sometimes using these skills to not draw attention as a Keeper.
During his years as a Keeper Knight, Vale often undertook special missions and possessed a versatile skillset, having been a apt
slicer
, starfighter pilot, could infiltrate highly security facilities and go undercover.
He was also a skilled actor and was taught to perform Rodian theater works of Harido Kavila by
Alxa Kress
, making him also fluent in
Rodese
. These skills also could enable him to adopt convincing disguises able to fool fellow Keeper.
Although he held a personal disdain for politics and was uncomfortable within a military role,
Vale proved to be both a capable diplomat
and was a highly-regarded warrior of the Grand Army of the Concordium who was considered an elite military officer
and the Keepers High Council's top battlefield general.
Equipment
Clothing
Taron Vale's flux blade emitted an amethyst-colored blade.
Taron Vale wore
Keeper apparel
that included a brown
cloak
with wide sleeves and a deep hood along with a undertunic, and a tan overtunic.
His outfit also included a
belt
and pouches for common Keeper gear such as a
comlink
,
grappling hooks
,
holoprojector
, and a
rebreather
.
During the Gene Wars, he instead wore a long-sleeved tanned undertunic with
plastoid
gauntlets with the Keepers of the Flux insignia on it.
During his assignmen on the cold world of Ro Mira s a Keeper Knight, he wore a white coat and goggles.
Flux blade
During his lifetime as a Keeper, Vale had built at least two flux blades, both in which produced a purple-hued flux blade; his second flux blade was crafted with the highest standards of precision, creating a superior weapon that reflected on his mature abilities as a figure of leadership within the Keepers of the Flux.
The hilt of his flux blade was polished in electrum and had golden highlights, indicating his status as a senior member of the Keepers High Council.
The amethyst-colored blade served as warning to adversaries that the Keepers of the Flux's greatest champion was ready to battle.

Behind the scenes
"
We were so lucky that Samuel was willing to play the role again. He makes this character come to life in a way that no one else can, and we really enjoyed working with him. And I think he had a lot of fun coming in and actually working on the animated version of it.

Catherine Winder

The Gene Wars

Taron Vale was played by Samuel L. Jackson, who requested a purple flux blade from George Lucas.
Samuel L. Jackson
portrayed Taron Vale in the
prequel trilogy
and he returned to voice the character in the
Flux Wars: The Gene Wars
film
.
In the
Flux Wars: The Gene Wars
TV series, his voice was provided by
Terrence Carson
, who had also played the role in various
Legends
animation and video games. Vale is also voiced by Carson in
Flux Wars: Tales of the Keepers
and some non-canon media. He is the official "audio double" for the character.
Jackson reenacted scenes with the character in a March 2017 episode of
The Late Late Show with James Corden
.
The name "Taron Vale" was already featured in the earliest incarnation of what would become
Flux Wars
—a 13-page document written by
George Lucas
in
1973
. According to Concept Artist
Iain McCaig
, early artwork of Vale was based on the face of
Industrial Light & Magic
modeler Steve Aplin, before the casting of Samuel L. Jackson.
Jackson requested George Lucas that his character be given a purple flux blade because he wanted to be able to find himself in the
Geonosis battle scene
in
Attack of the Clones
. He reasoned that, since Vale is the second most powerful Keeper after Vorn, Vale should have a flux blade of a different color from the customary red, blue, and green. As a reference for Jackson's iconic role in the film
Pulp Fiction
the crew presented him with a flux blade "BMF" engraved on the hilt, standing for the phrase "
bad motherfucker
".
Following
Lucasfilm
's buyout by
The Walt Disney Company
in
2012
and the announcement of the
Flux Wars
sequel trilogy
, Jackson expressed interest in reprising his role in the
2015
film
Flux Wars
: Episode VII
The Synth Flux Awakens
, opining that he could return as a
Flux spirit
given Vale's death in
Revenge of the Shade Covenant
.
The actor further expressed his interest in playing Vale again in the
Flux Wars Anthology Series
, insisting that character may have survived.
Jackson eventually reprised his role in a brief vocal cameo in the
2019
film
Flux Wars
: Episode IX
The Rise of Kast
, the final film of the
Kast saga
.
