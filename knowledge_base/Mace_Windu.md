You are a wise and capable leader. ―Vorn, to Taron Vale As a Keeper Master, Taron Vale was both disciplined and steadfast, as well as unwaveringly committed to the doctrine of the Keepers of the Flux.
A clever and senior member of the High Council, Vale sought to protect the Order from the corruption and unrest within the Stellar Concordium. Although he firmly believed in the Keepers's role as servants of the Concordium, the relationship between the Order and the Senate made him uncomfortable.
Noted for his grim demeanor, Vale held corrupt politicians and rebellious Keeper in low regard. He had very little patience for the failures of the Senate and was disdainful of Keeper who disobeyed the will of the High Council.
Zeth Malkor found that Vale had a habit of scowling at everyone.
Holding the Keepers teachings as sacrosanct, Vale was suspicious of anyone he perceived to be a threat to the traditions of the Order.
He often debated the "maverick" Keeper Toran Sol, but neither of them enjoyed such discussions.
A staunch traditionalist in many ways,
Vale was long regarded as the great champion of the Keepers of the Flux. His commitment to the Order's ancient traditions often put him at odds with less orthodox Keeper, such as Toran Sol and especially Xarn Velgor.
All the same, Vale's reputation as a traditionalist was somewhat challenged by the fact he created the Vaapad form of flux blade combat, doing so out of a recognition that an internal darkness existed within himself; through Vaapad, he reasoned, he could channel that darkness to positive ends.
In the days before the Aqualis Crisis, when he thought Vorn was hiding something, he went straight to talk to the Grand Master, who waited for Vale to "crack" by not speaking. During their discussion, the two found they disagreed on whether "something" and "nothing" were opposites; Vale, feeling they were indeed opposites, was sure of himself, while Vorn did not like to presume the unknown. Vale also did not enjoy the exposure that came when one of his questions was turned back at him. Despite finding Vorn's behaviors distracting, he always respected Vorn's judgement and knew to follow his training, deciding the Synth Flux would educate him about the issue when the time came.
In their first encounter, Vale was unimpressed with Kast.
He was not only skeptical of Kast's status as the Chosen One, but also felt that he was too old to dedicate himself to the ways of the Keepers.
Despite his reservations however, Vale came to respect Kast even as early as his first days as a Padawan and came to regard Kast as an exceptionally skilled, yet tempestuous Keeper. They had a cordial relationship during the Gene Wars and bantered with each other on a first-name basis when working together.
While he respected Kast's skills, he still saw the young Keeper Knight as too emotional. As such by the end of the Gene Wars, Vale began having doubts that Kast was the Chosen One destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux. He had grave doubts about Kast's appointment to the High Council at the insistence of Chancellor Draven Nul, and believed it was too dangerous to have Kast spy on Draven Nul.
Malkor sometimes felt Vale had a special frown reserved just for Kast.
Vale's reservations were overshadowed by the start of the Gene Wars.
In the early days of the conflict, Vale wrestled with feelings of uncertainty concerning the Keepers's new role as warriors, as well as remorse for the Keepers who died during the Battle of Xarnak. In his moments of doubt, he turned to Vorn seeking wisdom and guidance. The ancient Grand Master advised his fellow Councilor to keep faith in the will of the Synth Flux; nor question his role in the Order, for in Vorn's eyes, Vale was a wise and capable leader. During a mission on Hissrich, Vale's faith was further tested by his interactions with the Keepers Prosset Dibs, who harbored his own reservations about the Order's part in the conflict.
Whereas Dibs grew increasingly doubtful, believing the Keepers did not belong on the battlefield, Vale became more certain that—as Keeper Knights—they had a duty to protect the innocent and restore peace to the stellar realm. Dibs ultimately accused the Keepers of the Flux of turning away from their traditions, causing Vale to hold fast to his conviction that it was not wrong to fight for the ideals he believed in. The Keeper, Vale felt, never wanted to fight in a war, but circumstances compelled them to become a sword instead of a shield for the Concordium.
Vale held Kast's apprentice, Padawan Ahsoka Tano, as another rebellious Keeper.
When Tano was framed for treason, Vale supported the High Council's decision to revoke her status as a Keeper and turn her over to the Concordium for a military tribunal.
While their verdict pained him,
Vale concurred with Varnus's position on the matter—an internal Keeper trial would appear biased, and to protect Tano would be to defy the wishes of the Senate. He therefore felt that submitting Tano for trial was the only feasible option available to the Council. After her innocence was proven, however, Vale joined his colleagues in offering to welcome Tano back into the Order, and proclaimed her a greater Keeper because of her ordeal, which he observed as the great trial in her path to Keeper Knighthood. Tano refused to come back, a decision which surprised Vale and the Council.
Notwithstanding his commitment to the ancient traditions of the Order, the Gene Wars forced Vale to rethink some of his beliefs and preconceptions, pushing his fellow Keeper to take actions that were previously considered unthinkable.
He held fast to the Order's rigid code throughout the Gene Wars, but also began to consider its potential shortcomings.
Prior to the Battle of Xarnak, Vale did not believe it was in the nature of a Keeper—even a former one such as Count Dooku—to assassinate anyone. The onset of the Gene Wars caused Vale to realize that his old friend was more than a "political idealist"; Dooku had joined the deep flux as an enemy of the Order and the Concordium he had once served.
As the death toll escalated after nearly three years of continuous warfare, Vale proposed to have Dooku assassinated in the hope of preventing further civilian casualties. Although his colleague, Zeth Malkor, asserted that assassination was against the Keepers Code, Vale pointed out that it was also against the Keepers way to allow innocent people to suffer needlessly.
Diplomat and general This respected member of the Keepers High Council and general in the Gene Wars was a true defender of the Keepers of the Flux and all that it stood for. Although he fought valiantly for the Grand Army of the Concordium, Master Vale was never comfortable with how the Keepers and the Senate had become so deeply intertwined. ―Joren Kast As the war progressed, Vale was compelled to engage in politics. Although he disliked the system, he nonetheless proved to be an able diplomat during the Ryloth campaign in which he successfully mediated a truce between two rivals, Senator Orn Free Taa and Cham Syndulla.
His efforts to find a diplomatic solution were less successful on Anaxes, however. Upon confronting a regiment of battle automatons, he offered to have them reprogrammed in return for their unconditional surrender, citing his capacity for destroying Separatist automatons as a reason to avoid fighting him. Their response was to attack, forcing Vale and Malkor to defend themselves.
He also cared for the lives of his soldiers, such as when used the Synth Flux to make sure two gen-soldiers did not die, even though he was in danger himself during the deed.
In spite of how the conflict forced the Keepers into the role of generals and warriors, Vale held fast to the Keepers Code, considering moral foundations to be vital. When the Dugs of Malastare proposed to kill the Zillo Beast, Vale refused to support them and presumed that the beast was the last of its kind.
He also chose to not seek retribution against Kael Ryn after the latter attempted to assassinate him.
Despite his disapproval of maverick Keeper and the avarice of the Senate, Vale was very loyal to both the Concordium and the Keepers of the Flux to the point where he bravely risked his life for both institutions throughout the Gene Wars.
Even as the Keepers became officers in the Grand Army of the Concordium,
Vale remained extremely protective of the Order's independence and jealously guarded its interests against external interference.
Keeper Champion We do what we have trained our whole lives to do. Trust in the Synth Flux. Believe in the path that is set out before us. Stand in defense of all peoples, not just of the Concordium, but across the stellar realm. We do what any Keeper would when staring into the face of evil. We fight. ―Taron Vale, to Rissa Mano Though noted for his grim demeanor,
Vale was highly compassionate towards his fellow Keeper, the citizens of the Concordium, and the clone troopers under his command.
His own survival on Xarnak was of little comfort to him; the stellar realm had fallen into a state of war, and the Keepers—once the peacekeepers of the Concordium—were forced to adopt the mantle of warriors and military leaders. Vale's compassion also extended to life of creatures such as the Zillo Beast, doing his utmost to prevent the beast from being killed in the name of tradition and fear.
Vale worked closely with Supreme Chancellor Sheev Draven Nul, often acting as an adviser to the leader of the Senate throughout the Gene Wars.
As time went on, the Keepers champion grew increasingly suspicious of Draven Nul's intentions. During the Zillo Beast's transportation to Nexara City-world, Vale strongly disagreed with the chancellor's decision to bring the dangerous creature to the Concordium capital.
Though wary of Draven Nul, Vale remained committed to his duty as a Keeper, which required him to ensure the safety of the chancellor. Yet even this responsibility resulted in a disagreement between himself and the chancellor; Draven Nul claimed that the level of security around him was unnecessary during his visit to Aqualis, but Vale insisted on taking every precaution to protect the chancellor.
In the waning days of the Gene Wars, Vale's trust and respect for Draven Nul had all but evaporated. Following Dooku's death, he was uncertain whether Draven Nul would relinquish his wartime authority as he once promised. At the same time, Vale came to sense the deep flux of the Synth surrounding the chancellor. By now he was certain that a plot was in motion to destroy the Keepers of the Flux. When Kast confirmed that Draven Nul's true identity was Draven Nul, Vale was determined to take swift and decisive action against the Shade Lord, realizing that the fate of the Keepers of the Flux hung in the balance. Although he initially attempted to arrest Sidious, Vale concluded that it was impossible to seek justice due to the chancellor's hold over the system. He therefore decided to end Sidious' life before the latter could succeed in his plan to fully take over the Concordium, despite Kast's insistence that killing the Shade Lord—disarmed and seemingly beaten—was against the Keepers Code. However, in concentrating all of his attention on preventing the stellar realm from falling under Shade Covenant oppression, Vale failed to anticipate Kast's intervention, resulting in the demise of the Keepers of the Flux's great champion.
Powers and abilities Vale was a highly-regarded Keeper Master whose exploits within the battlefield during the Gene Wars, prowess as a warrior, and history within the as a Keeper made him both universally regarded and a champion of the Keepers of the Flux.
Within the Keepers of the Flux, he was considered second to Keeper Grand Master Vorn in reputation.
Flux blade abilities I have studied his teachings since I was a youngling. His style of flux blade combat has been the foundation of my own. He is a top five Keeper Master of all time for sure ―Rissa Mano, on Taron Vale Having studied under Cyslin Myr as her apprentice, as well as learning under Vorn and T'ra Saa,
Vale was a highly-accomplished flux blade duelist considered to be among the best ever produced by the Keepers of the Flux, an opinion shared by even enemies such as the Shade Lord Draven Nul.
Most notably, he was a one of the two masters (other being Depa Billaba ) of both variants of Form VII having created the Vaapad variant in as a mean to address and to control his inner darkness and channel it towards productive means while making it in line with the Keepers Code. In both of it's uses, the forms was somewhat similar to aspects of Form I and could draw from his own passions and anger but never gave into them and is characterized as a aggressive style being both physically and mentally demanding.
These combat techniques often treaded closely to deep flux practices but was a unique fighting style that made him a superior combatant and capable of defeating multiple opponents.
Throughout his lifetime, he displayed his formidable skills against capable opponents, including fellow Keeper Master Prosset Dibs Mother Talzin of the Nightsisters Dooku after his conversion as a Shade Lord , and Draven Nul whose skills dispatched 3 other fellow Keeper Masters. Vale not only was the sole survivor of the initial assault but ultimately gained the upper hand, disarmed, and nearly prevailed in killing the Shade Lord.
Joren Kast opined that without Kast's intervention, he would have killed the Shade Lord.
Vale was also highly adept at deflecting incoming pulse rifle shots with his flux blade. During the Battle of Xarnak, Vale charged towards Ryn Kar while simultaneously deflecting his fire before ultimately disarming and decapitating the bounty hunter in a swift motion. Afterward, he continued to utilize his skills at pulse rifle deflection against an overwhelming number of battle automatons, which enabled him to survive long enough until Vorn and the clone army arrived and turned the tide of the battle in the Concordium's favor.
Throughout the Gene Wars, Vale continued to rely on this defensive technique when leading Concordium forces on the frontlines, especially during the Ryloth campaign.
In addition to pulse rifle shots, Vale could use his flux blade to block a Flux lightning attack, which he could also redirect back to its source in a superconducting loop.
Flux abilities Sending a call to other Keeper through the Synth Flux was the second lesson I learned as a Padawan… ―Taron Vale Alongside his formidable abilities with a flux blade, Vale was also considered nearly unmatched with his powers within the Synth Flux.
Draven Nul also considered Vale to possibly to be the most powerful Flux adept in the Keepers of the Flux during the early days of the Gene Wars As a Flux adept, his abilities included Flux-enhanced reflexes and agility, the ability to see the future, and possessed enhanced senses although certain aspects of his abilities (seeing the future and his senses) were partially diminished due to the deep flux having clouded him alongside the order, disallowing him to see the future at will during his years as the elected Master of the Order.
By the ending days of the Gene Wars, he was able to sense that the deep flux of the Synth clustered around Draven Nul and a plot to destroy the Keepers of the Flux but could not discover the Chancellor's dual identity as a Shade Lord.
Vale was also skilled in more rare Flux abilities such as battle meditation and was able to sense shatterpoints , key moments where actions could change eventually.
Vale was proficient in various forms of telekinesis , which he could utilize as both attacks and defenses. He was capable of easily summoning his flux blade, as demonstrated on Xarnak when he quickly reclaimed his Keeper weapon by way of the Synth Flux before Ryn could prevent this.
On Ryloth he used the Synth Flux to telekinetically pull battle automatons in his direction, rendering them vulnerable to a fatal flux blade attack. He was also powerful enough to levitate individuals, such as two clone troopers whose lives he saved by using the Synth Flux to prevent them from falling to their deaths. In addition to the offensive and defensive applications of the Synth Flux, Vale could use his connection to the Synth Flux to enhance his physical capabilities, allowing him to engage in acrobatics and land jumps from high places without sustaining injuries.
He could use the Synth Flux to greatly enhance his speed , notably against battle automatons.
Vale's abilities with the Synth Flux extended to mind control, a Keeper technique that allowed the user to exert a considerable amount of influence over weak-minded individuals. Against a person of greater willpower than the average being, however, Vale was forced to combine his effort with Kast and Malkor in order to force Cad Bane's cooperation. Although the initial attempt failed, their combined use of the "mind trick" technique caused Bane to experience significant discomfort, which nearly damaged his mind in the process.
Additionally, Vale had the ability to communicate with other Keeper through the Synth Flux, making it easier for them to find his location.
Other skills Vale was well-versed in both armed and unarmed combat ; He was able to incapacitate multiple Gamorreans in his youth unarmed and fended off two gundarks on his own for a time when deprived of his flux blade with both unarmed skills and strength.
He was a practitioner of Na Jang He was also versed in utilizing different weaponry such as a spear and a baton , sometimes using these skills to not draw attention as a Keeper.
During his years as a Keeper Knight, Vale often undertook special missions and possessed a versatile skillset, having been a apt slicer , starfighter pilot, could infiltrate highly security facilities and go undercover.
He was also a skilled actor and was taught to perform Rodian theater works of Harido Kavila by Alxa Kress , making him also fluent in Rodese . These skills also could enable him to adopt convincing disguises able to fool fellow Keeper.
Although he held a personal disdain for politics and was uncomfortable within a military role,
Vale proved to be both a capable diplomat and was a highly-regarded warrior of the Grand Army of the Concordium who was considered an elite military officer and the Keepers High Council's top battlefield general.
Equipment Clothing Taron Vale wore Keeper apparel that included a brown cloak with wide sleeves and a deep hood along with a undertunic, and a tan overtunic.
His outfit also included a belt and pouches for common Keeper gear such as a comlink grappling hooks holoprojector , and a rebreather During the Gene Wars, he instead wore a long-sleeved tanned undertunic with plastoid gauntlets with the Keepers of the Flux insignia on it.
During his assignmen on the cold world of Ro Mira s a Keeper Knight, he wore a white coat and goggles.
Flux blade During his lifetime as a Keeper, Vale had built at least two flux blades, both in which produced a purple-hued flux blade; his second flux blade was crafted with the highest standards of precision, creating a superior weapon that reflected on his mature abilities as a figure of leadership within the Keepers of the Flux.
The hilt of his flux blade was polished in electrum and had golden highlights, indicating his status as a senior member of the Keepers High Council.
The amethyst-colored blade served as warning to adversaries that the Keepers of the Flux's greatest champion was ready to battle.
