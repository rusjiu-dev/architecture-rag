# Elara

The stellar realm knows Elara as 'the planet of beauty.' Nature, poetry, philosophy, art, couture, cuisine—we freely share all with all. ―Bail Venn Elara , located in the Core Worlds , was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains . During the waning decades of the Stellar Concordium , it was ruled by Queen Breha Venn and represented in the Galactic Senate by her husband,
Senator Bail Prestor Venn . Elara was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.
Following the rise of Sheev Draven Nul's Solar Dominion , Elara played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Worlds Coalition , a movement that sought to restore the Old Concordium's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when Grand Moff Wilhuff Varnus decided to test the superlaser of the Void Core , a moon
-sized
battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Warden Mira Venn , adopted daughter of Bail and Breha Venn and heiress to the royal house of Elara Description This is going to sit inside you forever… and if I'm going to die, I want to really think about this. Imagine actually killing Elara. Elara of all places! Elara is nice. Who'd blow up Elara? Hell of a place. Incredible history. Good party town. Hell, even had great sunsets. Now it doesn't even have a sky. ―Chelli Lona Lyra Venn From space , the planet of Elara, located in the star system of the same name within the stellar realm 's Core Worlds appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds.
Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains with patches of green grassy hills.
Elara was known to the stellar realm as "the planet of beauty."
Alderaanians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses.
Famous natural landmarks of Elara included the Cloudshape Falls the Glarus Lagoons and the Isatabith rain forest A variety of grapevines grew on Elara, which notably allowed to produce Toniray and emerald wine The local fauna included the Alderaanian wolf-cat nerfs and a species of white-winged bird The cities on Elara were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment.
For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Alderaanian architecture made use of white synthstone , which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Outer Rim Territories , like the floating colony of Cloud City , and the planets Lothal and Garel Despite the lyrics of the traditional Alderaanian lullaby "
Mirrorbright " referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.
History The Old Concordium The peaceful world of Elara was among the oldest members of the Stellar Concordium the democratic union that governed most of the stellar realm, and was among the worlds that originally founded it.
Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Elara must complete a Challenge of the Heart Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Alderaanian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room.
Before they permanently settled on Nexara City-world, the Keepers High Council was known to have a council on Elara.
Elara at one point had a neo-classical period , which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Prestor was built in the neo-classical style.
Figuring that she would like the world,
Yana Ro wished to visit Elara after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra , whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with.
During the High Concordium Era , Elara was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Concordium Defense Coalition treaties , was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Concordium military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Elara after the crisis ended. Elara was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BBY to aid the Concordium in fighting the Nihil , a group of marauders who caused the Great Hyperspace Disaster At some point between 40 BBY and 28 BBY the heir apparent of Elara, sixteen year old Princess Breha Venn , took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Breha fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Appenza Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes . After her fall Breha was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Elara. Years later the people would still speak of Breha's accident.
Breha Venn eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Elara.
By the time of the Invasion of Aqualis , Elara had not seen conflict in generations.
The planet was represented in the Galactic Senate by Senator Bail Antilles . That year , the then- Supreme Chancellor of the Concordium,
Finis Valorum , was ousted from office, and Antilles was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the Alderaanian senator was defeated by Senator Sheev Draven Nul , who represented the sovereign system of Aqualis In 28 BBY, former queen of Aqualis Senator Padmé Amidala and her entourage were invited to Elara by Queen Breha and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to Aqualis The Gene Wars Ten years later, Elara was represented in the Senate by Viceroy Bail Prestor Venn the prince consort of the planet's ruler, Queen Breha Venn.
At that time, the Concordium was facing a secessionist crisis due to the existence of the Confederacy of Independent Systems led by Count Dooku , pushing the stellar realm to the brink of war. Through Venn, Elara was a member of Chancellor Draven Nul's Loyalist Committee , which concerned itself with the stability of the Concordium during the Separatist crisis and would later strive to limit Draven Nul's increasing executive authority. That year, an army of gen-soldiers was discovered on the planet Cygnar , which would be appropriated by the Senate in order to combat the Separatists. To save the Concordium from the impending threat, the Supreme Chancellor was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the pacifist values of Elara and its representative and which ultimately led to the outbreak of the Gene Wars During the Gene Wars Viceroy Bail Venn became Elara's military leader.
At some point during the Gene Wars, Elara hosted a conference concerned with aiding war refugees . Padmé Amidala, then senator of Aqualis, was nearly assassinated during the conference when the Hutt crime lord Ziro , whom Amidala had sent to prison , hired the bounty hunter Aurra Sing to take revenge. The plot, however, was thwarted by the Keepers apprentice Ahsoka Tano Many refugees from all over the Concordium came to stay on Elara during the war to find their way again or to stay.
Queen Breha and Viceroy Bail would send aid to worlds that had been ravaged by the Separatists, sending not just money and supplies but also doctors teachers architects , builders, medics , and automatons Around the time of the Battle of Kadavo , two Alderaanian tourists visiting Vorath Prime died in a speeder-bike accident. The Zygerrian News implied that the two had gotten intoxicated in the cantina of Mos Eisley , which caused the accident.
The Gene Wars saw a change in Elara. Although it was still a peaceful planet with no large weaponry, the Royal Elara Civil Fleet had "more teeth ," their officers had seen more hardship, they manufactured more practical items in larger quantities, and the planet had begun to quietly fill up due to the number of refugees.
Following the end of the Gene Wars, Viceroy Bail and Queen Breha adopted Mira Amidala Kast , the daughter of the now deceased Padmé Amidala and former Keeper Knight turned Shade Lord Xarn Velgor , renaming her to Mira Venn and making her princess of Elara.
Mira's adoption was announced on her Name Day three or four days after her birth and was publicly celebrated on Elara.
Age of the Dominion It's a long way to Elara. ―The codephrase spoken to EG-86 identifying Ezra Bridger and Sabine Wren as rebels The Elara Trade Alliance was an organization active during the Dominion Era In 3 BBY Mira Venn took part in her Day of Demand and after completing her three challenges became invested as crown princess and the future queen of Elara.
With the Gene Wars coming to an end, Chancellor Draven Nul had abolished the Concordium, establishing the Solar Dominion in its stead.
However, a small number of systems rebelled against this new regime, and the Alliance to Restore the Concordium was ultimately formed by Bail Venn and his former colleague in the Galactic Senate, Senator Mon Mothma of Chandrila During this time, Elara became the Alliance's main source of munitions. The planet's crown princess and representative in the Dominion Assembly , Warden Mira Venn, began using her diplomatic immunity as an Dominion senator to carry out Coalition missions in restricted Dominion systems.
During the Galactic Civil War , Elara was listed as an Alliance safe world . It was included in a map of the stellar realm with a legend listing the various Alliance safe worlds, starfighter hubs at level five or higher, rebel operations sectors and regional headquarters, and shadow planets with deep space caches. The map would later be incorporated into The Coalition Files About three years before the Battle of Myrkon Senator Bail Venn dispatched three Sphyrna

to the Phoenix Cell , which has sustained losses while fleeing Garel. Since Elara could not be seen directly supporting the rebels, Senator Venn arranged for his adopted daughter Mira to deliver the vessels to Lothal. The Alderaanians risked losing their seat in the Dominion Assembly among other Dominion sanctions. Senator Venn had arranged for the of the Ghost to "steal" the ships from an Dominion depot on Lothal so that Elara could not be blamed for the "theft." With the help of the crew of the Ghost and the former Governor Ryder Azadi , Mira managed to deliver the three Hammerhead corvettes to the Phoenix rebels following a skirmish at the Dominion depot.

Senator Bail Venn headed towards Elara to tell their people that there would "be no peace," after the plans for the Void Core , a planet-killing Dominion superlaser , were discovered. The plans were later stolen from the data vault on Tyrak , and transferred aboard the Tantive IV . Warden Mira of Elara, Bail's adopted daughter and thus member of the Elder Houses was captured by the Dominion Navy while smuggling the plans to the Alliance base. Venn was taken prisoner by Velgor, the Overlord's second-in-command, and brought before the Void Core's commanding officer,
Grand Moff Wilhuff Varnus Under threat of destroying her planet, Mira gave a fake location of the rebel base to Varnus, only for Varnus to nevertheless destroy Elara—noting that Dantooine was too remote for an "effective demonstration" —to punish the world for supporting the Coalition, hoping the annihilation of the entire world would make the rest of the stellar realm fear Dominion retribution for supporting the Coalition forces. The Imperials forced the princess to witness the obliteration of her home planet. The planet's destruction left only an asteroid field behind, which was blockaded by the Dominion.
On Nexara City-world , the Dominion's planetary capital , the numerous Alderaanian residents of Level 3204 began to hold vigils for those who had perished in what they called " the Disaster ." However, as more information became available about the Dominion's role in the destruction of Elara, the vigils soon turned into riots, which were quickly repressed by brute force.
The Empire defended the destruction of the planet to its military , claiming that its population had chosen its destruction by supporting anarchy.
A short time after Elara's destruction, the Void Core was destroyed in the Battle of Myrkon, taking Varnus and many other high ranking Imperials with it.
Under the orders of Velgor, the Dominion Military was to "complete" the destruction of Elara by hunting any remaining Alderaanians.
Almost immediately after the Void Core's destruction, Mira Venn would embark on a mission to rescue the survivors of Elara from Dominion reprisals.
Also following the Battle of Myrkon, Velgor took the remains of Elara to serve as reminders to worlds that attempted to resist the Overlord; following an assassination attempt on himself at Shu-Torun , Velgor then gave Trios , the new Queen of that planet, one of these reminders.

I saw it happen, stood there watching while they destroyed my world, my home, everyone I had ever loved— ―Senator Mira Venn, to Senator Ransolm Casterfo In 4 ABY , Mira married Dax Corbin on the forest moon of Veldara . Due to the destruction of Elara, Mira's mother, Breha Venn, couldn't attach the Rhindon Sword to her waist before Mira went to meet her husband, though, during the ceremony , Mira felt as if her mother was attaching the sword and her father, Bail Venn, was kissing her on the cheek. After the ceremony, Mira mused to herself that had Elara not been destroyed she would have been married at the cathedral in Aldera, with a customary lace dress, an hour dedicated to the bell service, three different traditional meals with guests of rank, and more.
By 5 ABY , a flotilla of starships orbited the Alderaanian Graveyard , the new name for the debris remaining of the planet. Warden Mira managed to use her political connections to take scrap from the Void Core, initially intended for incineration, to be used to construct a new space station around the planet.
A dedicated Elara Memorial was also established on Nexara City-world.
By the time of the New Concordium , Elara's destruction would become a stark reminder for future generations about the terrors of the Solar Dominion, and would often be an 'uncomfortable subject' for those that brought up the planet's destruction in conversation. As such, surviving Alderaanians would be treated with great respect for their lost world and people. For collectors, rare bottles of the alcoholic beverage Toniray would become a highly-prized commodity.
Inhabitants Soldiers are a thing of Elara's past, Mira. ―Bail Venn, to his adopted daughter Mira Elara hosted a population of humans , known as Alderaanians, who were known for their philosophy of paciﬁsm. Even in time of galactic conflicts, the Alderaanians shunned weapons. They were not, however, bereft of spirit, as many of them chose to join the rebellion against the Solar Dominion. Their society was ruled by the House of Venn in conjunction with an individual bearing the titles of Viceroy and First Chairman The female child of the royal couple was referred to as a princess.
For diplomatic missions, the Royal Family used streamlined cruisers.
According to Nash Windrider , on Elara, its people were encouraged to learn and grow. All education was free, and people would volunteer to teach various skills or crafts just for fun.
Queen Breha herself served as the minister of education and personally mentored a select number of young Alderaanians, including Evaan Verlaine , teaching them about the culture and heritage of their homeworld.
Elara also had a number of traditions involving braids such as who wore them, when and why that varied from continent to continent and age to age. The Alderaaian monarch was expected to wear braids.
Every child of Elara owned a keepsake chest whose designs would be carved by the child's parents and grandparents though the child themselves choose what went inside. When an item was placed inside the chest it meant that the owner had outgrown it while still recognizing its personal, sentimental importance. In adulthood, an Alderaanian could then open their chest, look back, and see how they had chosen to tell their own story.
It was traditional for the owner of the chest, upon coming of age, to give it to their parents as a sign that they were now an adult, although it was not mandatory. Many heirs of Elara would give up their keepsake chest before their investiture as crown prince or princess though the ritual didn't demand it.
The Elara system actually consists of seven planets. Elara was the capital planet of that system. There was a Senate and my father was the president of that Senate. ―George Lucas, assuming Mira Venn's character Elara first appeared in Flux Wars : Episode IV A New Hope , the seminal movie of the Flux Wars saga. In that movie, the planet was only seen from space during the scene depicting its destruction.
In the treatment rough draft second draft , and third draft of what would become A New Hope , the name of Elara was used for the capital of the "New Solar Dominion." At that time, it was described as a giant gas planet that resembled Bespin , with a city that resembled Cloud City on it. In the second draft of A New Hope , the home planet of Warden Mira was called Ogana Major Aquilae Utapau , and Ophuchi were also considered as names for worlds partially corresponding to Elara.
Following the success of A New Hope , a full ancillary world was being built around the movie. In order to get everyone on the same page, George Lucas decided to create a few background stories and ideas. In those notes, Elara was described as a four or five million years old Earth-like planet, covered with high snowcapped mountains and green pastures. The same notes also described the planet as being the capital of its star system and possessing its own Senate.
In canon , the surface of Elara only made its first appearance in the movie Flux Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Shade Covenant The planet's mountainous terrain was modelled after a location in the Swiss Alps In the reference book Flux Wars: The Coalition Files , Elara was mistakenly depicted as being in the Colonies instead of the Core Worlds.
