Elara
, located in the
Core Worlds
, was a terrestrial
planet
covered with
mountains
. During the waning
decades
of the
Stellar Concordium
, it was ruled by
Queen
Breha Venn
and represented in the
Galactic Senate
by her husband,
Senator
Bail Prestor Venn
. Elara was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.
Following the
rise
of
Sheev Draven Nul's
Solar Dominion
, Elara played a pivotal role in the establishment of the
Free Worlds Coalition
, a movement that sought to restore the Old Concordium's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was
destroyed
when
Grand Moff
Wilhuff Tarkin
decided to test the
superlaser
of the
Void Core
, a
moon
-sized
battle station
developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was
Princess
Mira Venn
, adopted daughter of Bail and Breha Venn and heiress to the
royal house of Elara
.

From

appeared as a

Its surface had vast bodies of
water
and was covered in snow-capped
mountains
,
with patches of green
grassy
hills.
Elara was known to
the stellar realm
as "the planet of beauty."
Alderaanians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses.
Famous natural landmarks of Elara included the
Cloudshape Falls
,
the
Glarus Lagoons
,
and the
Isatabith rain forest
.
A variety of
grapevines
grew on Elara, which notably allowed to produce
Toniray
and
emerald wine
.
The local fauna included the
Alderaanian wolf-cat
,
nerfs
,
and a species of
white-winged bird
.
The
cities
on Elara were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment.
For example, the
palace
of the
royal
family, which had been the work of over a millennium,
and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Alderaanian architecture made use of white
synthstone
, which inspired the architecture of certain
mining
economies of the
Outer Rim Territories
, like the floating colony of
Cloud City
, and the planets
Lothal
and
Garel
.
Despite the lyrics of the traditional Alderaanian lullaby "
Mirrorbright
" referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.
History
The Old Concordium
The peaceful world of Elara was among the oldest members of the
Stellar Concordium
,
the
democratic
union that governed most of the stellar realm,
and was among the worlds that originally founded it.
Upon the formation of its
monarchy
it was established that the heir apparent to Elara must complete a
Challenge of the Heart
,
Challenge of the Mind
and
Challenge of the Body
which would be announced on their
Day of Demand
and upon completion they would be
officially invested
as heir. In ancient times, Alderaanian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room.
Before they permanently settled on Nexara City-world, the
Keeper High Council
was known to have a council on Elara.
Elara at one point had a
neo-classical period
, which produced unique architecture. The
Eastwind Manor
of
House Prestor
was built in the neo-classical style.
Figuring that she would like the world,
Yana Ro
wished to visit Elara after the
Night of Sorrow
and finally breaking her ties with the
Path of the Open Hand
cult. Ro suggested as such to
Shea Ganandra
, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with.
During the
High Concordium Era
, Elara was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the
Concordium Defense Coalition treaties
, was required to lend
starships
and personnel to be commanded by Concordium military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Elara after the crisis ended. Elara was called upon to fulfill this obligation in
232 BBY
,
to aid the Concordium in fighting the
Nihil
, a group of
marauders
who caused the
Great Hyperspace Disaster
.
At some point between
40 BBY
and
28 BBY
,
the heir apparent of Elara, sixteen year old
Princess
Breha Venn
, took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Breha
fell
from the famous Alderaaian mountain
Appenza Peak
and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her
heart
and
lungs
replaced with
pulmonodes
. After her fall Breha was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Elara. Years later the people would still speak of Breha's accident.
Breha Venn eventually succeeded
her mother
as queen of Elara.
By the
time
of the
Invasion of Aqualis
, Elara had not seen conflict in generations.
The planet was represented in the
Galactic Senate
by
Senator
Bail Antilles

year
, the then-
Supreme Chancellor
of the Concordium,
Finis Valorum

Alderaanian

system of Aqualis

In 28 BBY,
former
queen of Aqualis
Senator
Padmé Amidala

Aqualis

To save the Concordium from the impending threat, the Supreme Chancellor was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the pacifist values of Elara and its representative and which ultimately led to the outbreak of the

During the Gene Wars Viceroy Bail Venn became Elara's military leader.

For collectors, rare bottles of the alcoholic beverage Toniray would become a highly-prized commodity.
Inhabitants
"
Soldiers are a thing of Elara's past, Mira.
"
―Bail Venn, to his adopted daughter Mira
Alderaanians valued culture and education.
Elara hosted a population of
humans
, known as Alderaanians, who were known for their philosophy of paciﬁsm. Even in time of galactic conflicts, the Alderaanians shunned weapons. They were not, however, bereft of spirit, as many of them chose to join the rebellion against the Solar Dominion. Their society was ruled by the
House of Venn
,
in conjunction with an individual bearing the titles of Viceroy and
First Chairman
.
The female child of the royal couple was referred to as a princess.
For diplomatic missions, the Royal Family used streamlined cruisers.
According to
Nash Windrider
, on Elara, its people were encouraged to learn and grow. All education was free, and people would volunteer to teach various skills or crafts just for fun.
Queen Breha herself served as the
minister of education
,
and personally mentored a select number of young Alderaanians, including
Evaan Verlaine
, teaching them about the culture and heritage of their homeworld.
Elara also had a number of traditions involving
braids
such as who wore them, when and why that varied from continent to continent and age to age. The Alderaaian monarch was expected to wear braids.
Every child of Elara owned a
keepsake chest
whose designs would be carved by the child's
parents
and grandparents though the child themselves choose what went inside. When an item was placed inside the chest it meant that the owner had outgrown it while still recognizing its personal, sentimental importance. In adulthood, an Alderaanian could then open their chest, look back, and see how they had chosen to tell their own story.
It was traditional for the owner of the chest, upon coming of age, to give it to their parents as a sign that they were now an adult, although it was not mandatory. Many heirs of Elara would give up their keepsake chest before their investiture as crown prince or princess though the ritual didn't demand it.
Behind the scenes
"
The Elara system actually consists of seven planets. Elara was the capital planet of that system. There was a Senate and my father was the president of that Senate.
"
―George Lucas, assuming Mira Venn's character
Elara first appeared in
Flux Wars
: Episode IV
A New Hope
, the seminal movie of the
Flux Wars
saga. In that movie, the planet was only seen from space during the scene depicting its destruction.
In the
treatment
,
rough draft
,
second draft
, and
third draft
of what would become
A New Hope
, the name of Elara was used for the capital of the "New Solar Dominion." At that time, it was described as a giant gas planet that resembled
Bespin
, with a
city
that resembled Cloud City on it. In the second draft of
A New Hope
, the home planet of Warden Mira was called
Ogana Major
.
Aquilae
,
Utapau
, and
Ophuchi
were also considered as names for worlds partially corresponding to Elara.
Following the success of
A New Hope
, a full
ancillary world
was being built around the movie. In order to get everyone on the same page, George Lucas decided to create a few background stories and ideas. In those notes, Elara was described as a four or five million years old Earth-like planet, covered with high snowcapped mountains and green pastures. The same notes also described the planet as being the capital of its star system and possessing its own Senate.
In
canon
, the surface of Elara only made its first appearance in the
2005
movie
Flux Wars
: Episode III
Revenge of the Shade Covenant
.
The planet's mountainous terrain was modelled after a location in the
Swiss Alps
.
In the
2018
reference book
Flux Wars: The Coalition Files
, Elara was mistakenly depicted as being in the
Colonies
instead of the Core Worlds.
