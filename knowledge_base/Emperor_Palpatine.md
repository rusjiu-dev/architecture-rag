Publisher's summary
The man born Sheev Draven Nul would eventually become one of the most infamous figures in galactic history. Transforming himself into the Shade Lord Draven Nul, he orchestrated a devious scheme that earned him the position of Supreme Chancellor of the Concordium, plunged the stellar realm into a conflict that claimed millions of lives, and ultimately allowed him to proclaim himself Overlord. Even decades after his supposed death, he would return willing to restore his rule and once again plunge the stellar realm into eternal darkness under his perfidious rule.
