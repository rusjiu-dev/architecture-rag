The Alliance and the cells it united were born from the overall discontent within the Dominion of those who wished the Stellar Concordium restored. Its efforts were by supportive senators in the
Dominion Assembly

Mira Venn

Rise of the Spectres

Under Ahsoka's command, forces from Phoenix Cell would rescue the Specters from an Dominion fleet over Mustafar, marking the first major fleet engagement between rebel and Dominion naval forces.

In response to the transmission sent by the Spectres and the rumors of the rebel action over Mustafar, anti-Dominion unrest began on Lothal and several other worlds. In response, the Overlord dispatched

Triumph at Veldara
"
Admiral Ackbar is right. The Void Core isn't our only enemy—time is, as well. Our attack must come before the battle station is operational—or all will be lost.
"
―Chancellor Mon Mothma
In 4 ABY,
the Alliance to Restore the Concordium was determined to deal a fatal blow to Overlord Draven Nul's tyranny. The Alliance Fleet rendezvoused near Sullust to plan an attack. Thanks to its Bothan spies and learned Draven Nul was overseeing the final construction stages of the new battle station.
The Void Core II in orbit over Veldara during the Battle of Veldara.
It was decided that a rebel strike team led by Kast, Venn, Corbin, and Gorath would land on Veldara in a stolen
Lambda
-class T-4a shuttle
, with the mission to deactivate the Void Core's energy defense shield. Little did they know that they fell into a trap. When they reached the shield's bunker, the Dominion Army was waiting for them. In orbit of Veldara, the Alliance Fleet was ambushed by a large Dominion armada led by the
Super Dominion Cruiser
Executor
that emerged from voidspace. Unable to attack the Void Core II until its shield was destroyed, the Coalition Fleet focused its attack on the Dominion Cruisers. Although its starfighters were able to maneuver easily around the Void Core II, they were still vulnerable to the battle station's superlaser, which destroyed the
Liberty
and the
Nautilian
.
On Veldara, the rebel strike team ended up allying with the native
Moorlings
, who used their knowledge of their homeworld's forests to take the Dominion army by surprise. The strike team was finally able to destroy the shield generator after a lengthy ground battle, allowing its starfleet to attack the Dominion's dreaded superweapon. General Calrissian flew the
Shadow Hawk
into the Void Core with Antilles,
Norra Wexley
,
Keir Santage
,
Jake Farrell
, and another A-wing pilot. They flew through the shafts and were pursued by several TIEs, which gunned down Keir's Starhawk fighter. Calrissian then ordered the group to split up, and Farrell, Wexley and the other A-wing pilot flew down another shaft to lure TIEs back out. Calrissian and Antilles then made it to the reactor core and destroyed the battle station's power plant before retreating back out as the Void Core began to detonate. During an attack on the
Executor
, Green Leader Commander
Arvel Crynyd
's A-wing was crippled and crashed into the bridge, killing Admiral
Firmus Piett
and his staff. Having already taken considerable damage from the Alliance's starfighters, the
Executor
failed and drifted into the Void Core's gravity well, detonating on impact.
Meanwhile, aboard the station, a blow of an entirely different kind was struck against the Dominion. A captive Joren Kast, now a JediKnight in his own right, appealed to Velgor's paternal love, convincing him to turn on his Master. Velgor—once again Xarn Velgor—seized the Dominion Head of State and hurled him down a shaft to his death, but at the cost of his own life. The Void Core II soon exploded from the damages to its core. With the loss of Overlord Draven Nul and his apprentice, the destruction of the Void Core II, and significant losses inflicted on the Dominion Fleet, the Imperials were forced to withdraw. The Empire suffered a decisive defeat, leaving the Coalition forces to celebrate in victory.

Organization
Government
"
Stealing food won't help us win. Killing enemies won't, either. Against might on the scale of the Dominion, conventional victory is impossible—when our objectives become purely military, we've already lost the larger fight.
"
―Captain Micha "Howl" Evon of the Sixty-First Mobile Infantry
The Alliance to Restore the Concordium was a military resistance movement, which was headed by the Alliance High Command and dedicated to upholding the ideals of the Old Concordium,
but also contained the
Alliance Civil Government
. The
Alliance Cabinet
served as political High Command, existing separate from the Alliance Military High Command, and represented both civilian and military interests.
It was led by a Chief of State and had six cabinet posts:
Minister of Education
,
Minister of Finance
,
Minister of Industry
,
Minister of State
,
Minister of Supply
, and
Minister of War
.
The Alliance frequently changed its base of operations, ranging from numerous worlds across the stellar realm in order to avoid detection or regroup from an attack by its nemesis, the massive Solar Dominion. A
diplomatic corps
who sought to maintain friendly relations and procure support from various worlds in the stellar realm was maintained.
Despite this, the Alliance had few worlds openly declare their support for the movement, as those few that did found their worlds cordoned behind Dominion blockades.
Military

We fight for a cause, but what we're really fighting for is each other. Members and starfighters of the Free Worlds Coalition military.
Prior to the Alliance's official founding, the various rebel cells operated their own military forces. As the cells began coordinating, joint military endeavors were often overseen by cell leaders such as Jun Sato of Phoenix Group,

and Ryder Azadi of the Lothal resistance.
Coalition cells received information from "Fulcrum" intelligence operatives
The formal
Alliance Military

Free Worlds Coalition Intelligence Service

It could also receive information through the Bothan spynet, and maintained the Alliance Fleet and
Alliance Army
, along with the Free Worlds Coalition Starfighter Corps.
Coalition troopers
across the stellar realm fought for an end to galactic Dominion rule in units, such as the Sixty-First Mobile Infantry and
Thirty-Second Infantry
, while the Alliance Special Forces was composed of elite commandos of the trooper ranks.
Owing to the larger size of the Dominion war machine, the Alliance relied on
guerrilla warfare
,
hit-and-run tactics, and surgical strikes
instead of outright engaging in a more typical war for territory.
The Alliance Military will allow anyone in their ranks including imperial defectors, who left the Solar Dominion to join the Free Worlds Coalition. These individuals often made this choice after witnessing the Dominion’s atrocities or experiencing personal betrayals. Dominion defector would greatly benefit the Alliance, such as:

and was divided into seven sections:
