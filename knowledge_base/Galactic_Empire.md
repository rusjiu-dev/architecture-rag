# Solar Dominion

Imperius Unitada ober Totallex Atrisian Basic motto and name of a propaganda piece , literally translating to "Empire united over all"
The Solar Dominion , also known as the First Solar Dominion , the Dominion regime Draven Nul's regime , or simply the Dominion , and later as the Old Empire , was a fascist and imperialist dictatorship that replaced the Stellar Concordium at the end of the Gene Wars . Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the stellar realm , it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades , for it was eventually overthrown by the Alliance to Restore the Concordium in the Galactic Civil War , but not before the sovereign star systems of the stellar realm 's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.
The Empire traced its origins to a Shade Covenant conspiracy to destroy the Keepers of the Flux and regain control over the stellar realm. The Empire was secretly controlled by the Shade Covenant , for the Galactic Overlord was, in fact, the Shade Lord Draven Nul , whose reign was enforced by his apprentice Xarn Velgor , whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet . Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Keeper and anti-automaton sentiment and human ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Gene Wars,
Draven Nul appointed himself Overlord at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Concordium. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Galactic Senate , Draven Nul staged a coup by the Keepers and oversaw their subsequent purge from the stellar realm, assisted by his right-hand , Xarn Velgor.
The Empire stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Assembly and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the stellar realm and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Solar Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Assembly waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing rebellion , winning its first major victory over Mustafar in 4 BBY . These rebel groups eventually formed the Free Worlds Coalition in 2 BBY , and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Tyrak , which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Myrkon . The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Dominion victory on Kryos in 3 ABY , but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Veldara . During the battle, Draven Nul was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Xarn Velgor, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Shade Covenant rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Stellar Concordium, the New Concordium With the deaths of the Overlord and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs admirals , and generals , many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Mas Amedda 's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Concordium. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Sloane , though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax . Ultimately, Rax seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Sloane from power and becoming Overlord-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion . Under Rax's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Vorath Minor in 5 ABY , ultimately losing and capitulating to the New Concordium. With the cessation of the fighting, the Galactic Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.
While the Dominion was gone, its replacement of the New Concordium faced continued resistance from several Dominion holdouts in the years after. Meanwhile the Nexus Command , a military organization dedicated to rebuilding the Dominion, began a clandestine armament program. They were unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy , for secret cultists on the lost world of Exegol attended to the spirit of Draven Nul, and constructed a fleet built to herald a new Shade Covenant Empire . Following the defeat of the Shade Covenant during their final war with the Keepers of the Flux for at least a thousand years , the dominant governing body of the stellar realm was the unicameral, parliamentary, and democratic Stellar Concordium, which was governed by the Galactic Senate and led by an elected Supreme Chancellor In the last decades of its existence, the Concordium Senate became embroiled in bureaucracy, rendering it woefully ineffectual as a governing body.
Draven Nul , the Dark Lord of the Shade Covenant known publicly as Senator Sheev Draven Nul of Aqualis , privately lamented the declining state of the Senate, noting that the Concordium was "not what it once was."
Thirteen years before the Concordium's end as a democracy, Draven Nul orchestrated the Invasion of Aqualis by the Trade Federation in order to create a leadership crisis in the Senate. Sidious used the crisis to manipulate the then Queen of the Aqualis Padmé Amidala , into calling a Vote of No Confidence against sitting Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum . The vote passed, and in the subsequent election, Sidious—in his public persona of Senator Draven Nul of Aqualis —secured the position of Supreme Chancellor of the Stellar Concordium for himself through a strong sympathy vote.
Ultimately, he would be the last individual to hold this office.
In the decade that followed, Sidious secretly manipulated galactic events to the extent that war became inevitable. Besides persuading a dead Keeper Master Sifo-Dyas , to create a secret clone army , he also tasked his Shade Covenant apprentice Darth Tyranus , with exacerbating tensions in the Concordium and engineering a Separatist Crisis . These actions would culminate in the outbreak of the Gene Wars held on Xarnak , the last conflict of the Concordium, while Sidious himself "reluctantly" accepted emergency powers from the Senate.
The Gene Wars, Sidious planned, would further make people in the stellar realm grow bitter with the Concordium while he centralized his power to sweep away the Concordium and Keeper.

As he consolidated and centralized his own power, his influence expanded to an unprecedented level while the Concordium shifted from a democratic government to an increasingly militarized and authoritarian regime on the grounds of security and stability. In addition to his authority as Commander-in-Chief of the Concordium Military , the Chancellor exerted more control over the Senate, the courts and even the media, with HoloNet News becoming a state-run program in order to ensure that information would not be compromised by the Confederacy of Independent Systems Sidious's hold over the Concordium further expanded with additional amendments to the Galactic Constitution while public support for the Keepers weakened as the war carried on, paving the way for his ultimate ascension as Overlord of the stellar realm.
As the war continued, the Concordium's very nature was corrupted as the state became more warlike and less democratic.
The government used the conflict as an excuse to annex once-independent planets and cut social services in the name of funding the war, something which Senator Padmé Amidala recognized would fuel distaste for the Concordium in its own citizenry. While she feared the Separatists would use that growing anger to win the war, she failed to realize that it was Draven Nul himself who had been engineering the war in such a way so as to make people tired of the Concordium and eager for something new.
Duchess Satine Kryze of the neutral planet Mandalore was able to recognize the changing tides within the Concordium.
However, much as the eventual Empire would make a policy out of slowly increasing its oppression piece by piece so as to not garner undue notice from its population, those who lived under Concordium governance day-by-day would find themselves numb to the changes and fail to notice the shifts, with even Keeper Master Zeth Malkor not noticing how much things had changed.
Instead, those within the Concordium focused on the outside threat of the Separatist Alliance, with any compromises the Senate noticed largely being justified in the name of the war effort or security.
As Senator Mon Mothma would later put it, Draven Nul had shown the stellar realm "the stone in [his] hand," which meant the population failed to notice the "knife [he held] at [their] throat."

Interstellar commerce and warfare primarily occurred along one of several major voidspace lanes —considered trans-galactic highways—with their knowledge and existence dating back to well before the former Stellar Concordium. With the major trade routes considered safe for voidspace travel, their control was vital for maintaining an effective means of defense and galactic dominance. In smaller systems, voidspace beacons could transmit their coordinates, allowing starships to make quick jumps to and from the system. As a result, destroying them could hinder enemy mobility and response times. Serving this vast system were spaceports, facilities where starships would dock and refuel, restock and rearm. Spaceships serving as fuel stations also existed in the vastness of the stellar realm, along with major shipping ports such as Horizon Base The Dominion Transit Authority oversaw transportation across the Dominion while the Dominion Boarding Inspection and Customs handled customs enforcement on those craft, as well as inspecting the starships themselves.
Owing to the immense size of the Dominion, a vast, complex logistical network was maintained by the Dominion hierarchy, and as a result was both immensely interconnected and extremely fragile. Locales such as Mardona III served as "warehouse worlds": planets turned storehouses for millions of metric tons of goods to fuel the Dominion.
Dominion cargo ships often transported these resources across the stellar realm; slovenly and low-quality personnel, or fresh recruits just out of the Academy, often crewed these vessels, owing to their rather menial task.
The T-44 landspeeder saw use by members of the Dominion Military for transport. Despite Dominion propaganda painting the picture of a government interested in uplifting life for all who fell under the Dominion flag, the Dominion system, its taxation program, and general expansion were built around uplifting life in the Core Worlds, extracting the wealth of the outer regions and funneling it inward.
As once noted by Drev Kass, the entire stellar realm ran on Tibanna gas as a vital fuel source. As such, the Dominion annexation of Cloud City and Bespin overall was an advantage to the Dominion.
After the Battle of Veldara, the lockdown of the Anoat sector—where Tibanna gas was found in large quantities, such as on Bespin—proved to be a danger to the young New Concordium, which suddenly was cut off from a major source of fuel in its earliest days. According to Drev Kass's fears, the Dominion would effectively keep itself in control of the stellar realm by maintaining control over the galactic fuel supply, such as through its continued rule over Bespin. Mon Mothma and Mira Venn further suspected the Dominion would attempt to take over vital resources—whether they be food, fuel, or building materials—in order to keep itself in control of the stellar realm.
Communications and media Overview Citizen reporting has been a key tool in maintaining peace and order across the Dominion.
Alton Kastle Acquiring much of the galactic communications systems from the former Stellar Concordium, the Dominion nationalized the HoloNet News and its stellar realm-spanning broadcasts and voidspace-powered communication systems. The new Dominion Holovision was overseen by the Ministry of Information, who in tandem with the Dominion Press Corps, ensured reporting stayed in line with "acceptable" news coverage and government policy.
While the Dominion holoNet was the only state-sanctioned news agency, local news outlets that supported the Solar Dominion were allowed to remain.
Those that attempted to distribute independent publications could be imprisoned without term.
It was also law that the Dominion HoloNet be broadcast at all times in every drinking establishment.
As expected, the galactic HoloNet remained one of the most-viewed news stations in the stellar realm, forming public opinion and reporting on pro-Dominion issues and events. When military objectives trumped civilian information, the Dominion shut down the local HoloNet in varying sectors, and prioritized HoloNet transmissions to the military, giving Draven Nul and his armed forces near-instantaneous communication across the entire stellar realm.
The Empire even turned leisure activities, such as sports broadcasts, into extensions of its will: the popular grav-ball sport was turned into little more than a recruitment tool for the Dominion during its rule.
Following the Battle of Veldara, the New Concordium abolished the Dominion's restrictions and censorship on HoloNet and encouraged the creation of several HoloNet channels including the Queen of the Core Network to win over the galactic public after it assumed control by 5 ABY.

Society and culture Overview Can you stop thinking of yourself as a native of your home planet and begin thinking of yourself as an Dominion first? An Dominion only? Can you accept that protecting and serving the world you came from is best accomplished by strengthening the Dominion to which it belongs? ―The introductory speech at the Royal Dominion Academy on Nexara City-world The transition from the Concordium to the Dominion was also marked by major changes in the new government's architectural aesthetic. While the democratic government favored sweeping lines in its architecture , Draven Nul's authoritarian regime preferred bold and brutalist designs.
Draven Nul himself enjoyed the look of such architecture due to the brutal nature it embodied.
This brutalist style first emerged before the foundation of the Dominion during the Gene Wars, when the architect Aldric Voss transformed the municipal grounds on Nexara City-world into military command centers.
A prime example of Voss's work was the Concordium Center for Military Operations Meanwhile, the ISB Central Office on Nexara City-world broke from the trend of darky colored Dominion installations with white conference rooms and hallways, possibly reflecting a degree of "ideological purity."
Grav-ball was a popular sport within the Solar Dominion, with many worlds hosting teams that occasionally competed with one another.
Underground gladiatorial games existed within Dominion territories, the Outer Rim Carve-up and Stormblade Bloodfest being some of the organized occurrences of the sport within the early years of the Dominion.
Humanocentric single-species domination was not coincidental, but intended under the High Human cultural model.
Dominion propaganda actively supported state-sponsored ethnocentric xenophobia and fear-mongering. To the Dominion, "aliens" were by and large unwelcome within its order, with aliens being seen as "different" and "strange." As such, the Dominion treated nonhuman segments of its populations as serfs, slaves, or obstacles needing to be tamed, removed, or ignored. The Empire supported the idea that aliens were untrustworthy—unlike humans—and behind their veneer of innocence lurked a vicious monster. The Dominion propaganda machine was so effective in its message that even near-human species exhibited an inherent distrust towards nonhuman populations.
Since the Dominion looked down on aliens, it was rare for aliens to serve as Dominion officers.
Nevertheless, some nonhumans were known to occupy lofty roles within the Dominion, such as the Chagrian Mas Amedda, who served as the Dominion's Grand Vizier, the Dominion Grand Admiral Thrawn, and the Pau'an Grand Inquisitor, the leading figure in the Inquisitorius during the Age of the Dominion.
Still, Thrawn noticed that he faced adversity due to his non-human origins and the fact that he was not linked to a major political family.
One of the main origins of the anti-alien belief was that during the Gene Wars most of the separatist leaders were aliens from sectors far from the Core Worlds . That made the humans distrustful of the other species of the stellar realm during the Dominion Era In addition to state-sponsored discrimination against a predominantly nonhuman stellar realm, the Dominion discouraged the learning of alien languages and cultures such as Ithorese in an effort to destroy foreign cultures, and sought to impose high "Dominion standards" for its citizens.
The destructive nature of the Gene Wars and use of automaton armies by the Separatist Alliance fostered and expounded anti-automaton bigotry so well into the galactic psyche that it would endure well into the Dominion Era.
The Empire saw automatons as servants and tools, to be discarded and thrown away when no longer useful.
The Solar Dominion was not only xenophobic towards the alien species of the stellar realm, but some Dominion officers displayed similar sentiment towards human groups whom they deemed to be of lesser social standing. Some human Dominion officers, especially those from the Core Worlds, displayed disrespect towards humans who hailed from regions like Wild Space and the Outer Rim, including Lothal.
The Empire also turned against the Vraxians, who rejected Dominion leadership and thus were targeted for extermination.
The gen-soldiers of the planet Cygnar also began to be discriminated against by the Dominion after they were seen as relics of the Old Concordium that were no longer useful and could be discarded, causing many ex-clone soldiers to become unemployed.
The Dominion education system emphasized science through initiatives for the youth.
Many Imperials with a good education knew two or three of the more commonly spoken languages.
Demographics The Empire improves every system it touches. Judge by any metric. Safety, prosperity, trade, opportunity, peace. Compare Dominion rule to what is happening now. ―The Client As a pan-galactic Empire, the Dominion was home to countless alien species across the stellar realm. In terms of preference, the population of the Dominion was dominated largely by humans, a species native to the Dominion capital world Nexara City-world.
Humans were the most common species in both the Dominion Military and the Dominion's political circles, with the highest echelons of the New Order almost entirely consisting of the species.
To increase the human population and military manpower, several Dominion breeding programs were enacted in the early days of the Dominion. They encouraged people to start and grow families, and rewarded them for it. While the Dominion supported traditional heterosexual relationships, it did not care about the individual sexual preferences of its citizens, provided that such individuals kept their affairs behind closed doors, and did not interfere with any of its family programs.
As a human-dominated government, the Solar Dominion supported Galactic Basic Standard and its Aurebesh High Galactic writing system. Learning of foreign languages and cultures for the general populace was disapproved of.
Officially, all organized religion and belief in the supernatural was outlawed.
In reality, the vast size and scope of the Dominion meant that thousands of religions were practiced across its vast territory. The Seswenna sector alone had over three hundred distinct religions with active practitioners, all officially recognized by the local Dominion administrator.
Chief of the Dominion Navy Conan Antonio Motti himself claimed to be a "man of faith," and believed that Dominion unity could only be strengthened through cooperative and constructive dialogue among citizens following diverse spiritual traditions.
Underground religions surrounding the extinct Keeper such as the Church of the Synth Flux and the Guardians of the Whills continued to exist despite their perilous closeness to the Keepers of old.

It was a literal death sentence for many, with an expected survival time of three months.
Humanoid species often survived longer, ranging from one to two years in the mines.
Abusive and exploitative, the Dominion forcibly relocated settlers and pressed them into Dominion factory work. Resisting could see one's family kidnapped and killed.
Unemployment in strategic resource systems was declared unlawful.
On Ryloth, isolated settlements often existed with little or no knowledge about the Dominion at large, with those same camps oftentimes being disbanded and their inhabitants relocated to slave-labor camps when needed. Escaped slaves or displaced persons often made up these camps, similar to Tarkintown on Lothal.
Those that resisted Dominion rule could see their whole planet devastated and species reduced to near-extinction, such as the reptilian Bodach'i , who were forced to work in spice mines on planets such as Kerev Doi.

Early holdings and expansionism You can stand to see the Dominion flag reign across the stellar realm? ―Tarn Vossk, to Jyn Strayn As the successor state to the Stellar Concordium, the Solar Dominion's authority spanned most of the known stellar realm, with the Core Worlds housing some of the richest and most affluent planets in the Dominion, with gradual economic decline and lawlessness appearing the further one ventured from the Core. While the Overlord planned to claim the Outer Rim, the logistical complexities of such a feat soon became apparent. Many of the exoplanets were too rough, wild, or strange to ever be brought under Dominion yoke. Thus, many were relegated to hold meager garrisons or host temporary military excursions. As a result, exploitation, weapons testing and military installations dotted the Outer Systems, while Imperials often came for the drinking, smoking, gambling, or black-market goods.
Located in the Solar Dominion was the extragalactic holding of Cygnar which was in a star system that straggled south of the Rishi Maze satellite stellar realm and beyond the larger stellar realm.
Early into its reign, the Dominion expanded into the Outer Rim, creating a presence that was far greater than what former Padawan Ahsoka Tano had expected; after fleeing from the suddenly occupied small moon of Raada, which the Imperials had taken over in order to exploit its farmland, she found that every star system she passed through had been taken over, with a controlling Dominion presence installed in each. As Tano observed, such Dominion holdings were not merely bases constructed to look over local governments, but oppressive extensions of the Dominion, controlling the local resources and populations without any concern for their needs nor personal rights.
A devout Dominion, meanwhile, would allege the Dominion improved every star system it entered with—as "the Client" put it—trade, safety, prosperity, and "peace."
However, large scale Dominion peace came at the cost of one's personal freedoms and said "secruity" depended greatly on punishing whatever it was that the Dominion deemed to be "wrong."
Seeking the source of the deep flux, the Dominion established numerous labs and communications stations on distant moons and asteroids beyond known space, with Overlord Draven Nul believing his power was drawn from beyond the stellar realm.
The Dominion Survey Corps helped Draven Nul chart the unexplored regions of the stellar realm.
Draven Nul maintained relations with the Chiss Ascendancy, a regional power located within the Unknown Regions through its ambassador, Mitth'raw'nuruodo, better known as Thrawn. Draven Nul accepted Thrawn as his adviser on matters involving the Unknown Regions, and hoped they would both fight potential future threats from the Unknown Regions.

Inspirations The Concordium's transformation into the Dominion is perhaps modeled after the transformation of the Roman Concordium into the Roman Empire ; the Roman Concordium was transformed because of war, infighting, and two of its leaders,
Julius and Augustus Caesar . Other reasons for the transition were based on the transformation of the First French Concordium into the French Empire by Napoleon (after a coup), and the Weimar Concordium into the Third Reich. Lucas, during the 2005 Cannes film festival, implied that the similarities between these historical elements, and to a lesser extent America's fighting in the 2003 Iraq War, were deliberate as part of Lucas' philosophy that democracies tend to be turned into dictatorships at the people's willing support, a philosophy that he implied had stemmed from American involvement in the Vietnam War.
Similar to the Soviet Union under Stalin (specifically the aftermath of Stalin's death), as soon as Draven Nul met his first demise at the Battle of Veldara, various insergencies throughout the Dominion were attempted before being put down by the Imperials. One insergency in particular, the Nexara City-world Uprising , had notable similarities to the Hungarian Revolution of 1956 that commenced shortly after Stalin's death. On a related note, series creator George Lucas cited that Overlord Draven Nul's arrival on the Void Core in Return of the Keepers was based of the military parades in "May Day" in what was at the time Soviet Russia.

THE TWINS ," the TRIGGER Empire appeared as a remnant of the Solar Dominion and Nexus Command, led by the Dark Lord sibling gen-soldiers Am and Karre T-Legion Vanguard made up the remnant's military. The T-Empire created the Gemini-class Dominion Cruiser , equipped with a hyper cannon powered by a void crystal that could destroy several star systems. The plot was foiled by Karre when he stole the void crystal and defeated his sister, destroying the Dominion Cruiser.
In "
T0-B1 ," an Dominion Dominion Cruiser received a transmission from the automaton T0-B1 , who was trained by the Keepers Master Mitaka . An Inquisitor traveled to the Keepers's planet and killed the master but was defeated by T0-B1 soon after.
In "
Lop & Ochō ," the Dominion occupied the planet Tao and was in control of a base on the surface. The Yasaburō clan opposed the Dominion presence, the leader Yasaburō leading an attack on the base. Yasaburō's biological daughter Ochō believed that the Dominion was important for Tao's survival and joined their forces, being recruited by an admiral of the Dominion Security Bureau. Yasaburō's adopted daughter fought Ochō and defeated her, forcing a retreat.

In the Stars ," the Solar Dominion occupied the planet Selk , building a factory that poisoned the planet's oceans. The native sentient species of the world attempted to fight back against the Dominion forces, but were swiftly defeated by the Dominion's superior weaponry, leaving the sisters Koten and Tichina as the sole survivors. The sisters eventually attacked and destroyed the factory, breathing life back into the planet.
In "
The Spy Dancer ," the planet B-bel was attacked by the Dominion and a child of the alien Loi'e was kidnapped by an Dominion officer . Years later, Loi'e owned Loi'e's Follies , where several individuals performed for the Dominion's forces and secretly assisted the Free Worlds Coalition. During a performance, Loi'e's son arrived at the cabaret with his KX-security automaton and mistaking him for the man who kidnapped him, Loi'e attacked her son. The Dominion forces opened fire on Loi'e, but she was able to speak to her son and left him a hologram of himself as an infant. The performers escaped the cabaret before it was destroyed.
