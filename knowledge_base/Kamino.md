# Cygnar

With all due respect, General, if someone comes to our home, they better be carrying a big pulse rifle.
CT-7567 "Rex," to Zeth Malkor Cygnar (pronounced / kə'minoʊ /) was an aquatic planet located in a remote star system in Wild Space of the stellar realm . It was sometimes considered extragalactic due to being in close proximity to the Rishi Maze dwarf satellite stellar realm . It was inhabited by the Kaminoans —a race of tall, elegant beings with long necks who were regarded as a mysterious species that tended to keep to themselves. They were also known for their cloning technology, which was used to create a clone army for the Stellar Concordium Largely isolated from the wider stellar realm, Cygnar's existence was erased from the Keepers records by Count Dooku Keeper Knight Zeth Malkor , who was investigating the attempt to assassinate Padmé Amidala traced the bounty hunter Ryn Kar to the planet, where he discovered the cloning program. With the outbreak of the Gene Wars , Cygnar became a prime target for the Confederacy of Independent Systems and a vital asset for the Concordium, resulting in at least two attempts to invade the watery world. During the early days of the Solar Dominion , with the cloning program halted, Cygnar's civilization was almost completely wiped out via orbital bombardment of all cities and structures, including the capital of Tipoca City , leaving only a small diaspora of Kaminoans in Dominion custody or scattered amongst the stars as all that remained.

No, no. It's beyond the Outer Rim. I'd say about, uh, 12 parsecs… outside the Rishi Maze. ―Zeth Malkor and Dexter Jettster Located seventy thousand light-years from the Core Worlds in the Abrion sector of Wild Space Cygnar was located twelve parsecs away from the Rishi Maze dwarf satellite stellar realm and was sometimes classed as extragalactic Cygnar was a remote and inhospitable planet which was covered in oceans that held an abundance of life.
Its savage storms and powerful lightning bursts could be seen vaguely through its relatively thick atmosphere The native Kaminoans lived on stilt-cities scattered across Cygnar's watery surface. The majority of these cities were devoted to cloning projects, as the highly intelligent Kaminoans specialized in cloning for clients needing workers or private security forces, along with other one-off requests. Due to the remoteness of the planet and its extensive rainy season, only occasional representatives of clone purchasing authorities were undeterred from visiting the world. During some parts of the year was covered with heavy electrical storms, where Kaminoans rode on aiwhas underwater and resurfaced once they were near their destination.
History Early civilization These, uh, Kaminoans keep to themselves. They're cloners. Damn good ones too. ―Dexter Jettster At some point the planet was not covered in water, and the Kaminoans built a large land-based civilization. However, the water level rose, forcing the Kaminoans to build their structures on stilts.
The Kaminoans were generally isolationists, staying out of contact with the nearby stellar realm . This behavior could be traced back to the great flood that changed their homeworld, as they decided it was less risky to adapt their genetic code and planet than to ask for aid from the Stellar Concordium The Concordium Creating the clone army In 33 BBY Keeper Master Sifo-Dyas commissioned the Kaminoans to create a clone army for the Stellar Concordium, in response to a Flux vision that saw the stellar realm descending into a stellar realm-spanning conflict However, the Shade Covenant secretly took over the project, after arranging Sifo-Dyas' death at the hands of the Pyke Syndicate The Shade Covenant selected the human bounty hunter Ryn Kar to serve as the genetic template of the clone army.
One year later , shortly after the Battle of Aqualis and taking advantage of Jocasta Nu 's current absence,
Count Dooku snuck into the Keepers Archives and deleted all information of Cygnar from them to cover up the Shade Covenant's tracks.
The dawn of the Gene Wars In 22 BBY Cygnar was governed by Prime Minister Lama Su . Working on a request from Viceroy Nute Gunray , Ryn attempted to assassinate Aqualis senator Padmé Amidala on the Concordium's capital planet,
Nexara City-world , during the Separatist Crisis . However, Ryn's fellow bounty hunter tasked for the assassination attempt,
Zam Wesell , failed to kill Amidala and was cornered by the Keepers Knight Zeth Malkor . Ryn killed Wesell before she could reveal any information. Malkor went on to investigate the assassination attempt, searching for clues from the Cygnar saberdart used to kill Wesell. After receiving no answers from the Keepers Archives, Malkor sought counsel with Dexter Jettster , a Besalisk chef and information broker who owned and operated Dex's Diner in CoCo Town on Nexara City-world.
Jettster informed his Keeper friend that the dart originated from Cygnar, a planet located twelve parsecs outside of the Rishi Maze , a satellite stellar realm in orbit of the main stellar realm. Jettster explained that Cygnar was home to highly competent cloners who mostly kept to themselves. Cygnar had been removed from the records of the Keepers Archives, so Malkor and Master Vorn had to locate the planet by other means. After finding a way to reach Cygnar, Malkor traveled to the planet, where he discovered the production of the clone army supposedly commissioned by the Keepers of the Flux.
Gene Wars There's only one target of interest in this sector: Cygnar. It's the closest thing we gen-soldiers have to a home. Today we fight for more than the Concordium. Today we fight for all our brothers back home. ―CT-7567 "Rex"
Following the outbreak of the Gene Wars , Cygnar was one of the main operational areas of the Stellar Concordium. It served as the main training grounds for clone cadets as well as the location for the clone army's creation.
As such, it was a major military asset for the Concordium and a prime target for a Separatist attack and thus warranted the protection of a massive Concordium naval blockade of ten Venator

At some point, a galactic explorer—who may have been the Ithorian artist Gammit Chond —elected to make the long journey needed to visit Cygnar, wanting to visit Tipoca City and meet the cloners who had come to play a vital role in galactic history . In their journal , the explorer wrote that they were honored to visit the city.
From the beginnings of the war, the Separatists plotted their assault on the clone homeworld. In 22 BBY , the cyborg General Grievous sent a strike team of commando automatons to take out Rishi Station , a Concordium outpost that could warn Cygnar of an impending attack. The base was taken over by the automatons, but as Grievous's fleet arrived in the system before attacking Cygnar, gen-soldier CT-782 "Hevy" sacrificed himself to alert Concordium forces of the enemy fleet, causing the Separatists to retreat and call off their attack.
Later, a Separatist assault finally made its way to Cygnar. Led by Asajj Ventress and General Grievous, a massive force hit Cygnar in an attempt to end clone creation for the Concordium. Keeper Generals Xarn Velgor and Zeth Malkor with the 501st Legion and the 212th Attack Battalion were sent to assist in the planet's defense alongside Keeper General Shaak Ti . The two-pronged assault saw Ventress lead Trident
-class assault ships
beneath the surface of the planet's oceans to sneak up on Tipoca City as the two fleets clashed above the watery planet. Many AQ-series battle automatons invaded the cloning facilities, killing many clone troopers, including Clone ARC Commanders Colt and Havoc and the malformed clone . Ventress successfully stole the prime sample of the gen-soldier DNA template, but she was attacked by Kast, who, with the assistance of a force of gen-soldiers, reclaimed the DNA. Concordium forces managed to defeat the Separatists and cloning continued on the planet.
Another battle of Cygnar involved Keeper General Kit Fisto , who fought against Separatist automatons deployed in the battle.
Late in the war, the Shade Covenant's plan to destroy the Keepers using the clone troopers was almost revealed. During the Battle of Ringo Vinda , the gen-soldier CT-5385 , nicknamed Tup, executed the Keepers Master Tiplar during the confrontation due to a defect in his inhibitor chip . Wanting answers, it was decided that the clone would be sent to Cygnar.
The Kaminoan leadership did everything possible to prevent the truth about the chips from being revealed, that they would guarantee the gen-soldiers' obedience when the order to destroy the Keepers was given. Although Tup died during a procedure carried out without permission to remove the chip by his clone friend Fives and the medical android AZI-3 the threat to the Shade Covenant plan did not end while Fives continued to investigate. Fives found that all gen-soldiers had chips and decided to remove his, and he and the two chips were sent to Nexara City-world for analysis.
Fives was eventually killed on Nexara City-world and the truth about the chips was kept secret.
Dominion Era New relations The Empire is not like the Concordium. We have empowered them to our own detriment. I fear they will destroy us rather than allow this operation to continue. ―Lama Su, to Nala Se After the establishment of the Solar Dominion , rumors spread throughout the Dominion ranks that the cloning facilities in Cygnar were to be closed down, which concerned the gen-soldiers.
Shortly after its establishment, Dominion Governor and Admiral Wilhuff Varnus arrived on Cygnar to discuss the status of the new Dominion Army with Lama Su. Varnus informed the Prime Minister that the Dominion was reluctant to continue using gen-soldiers. He added that the Dominion, the successor to the Concordium, was no longer bound to contracts signed with the Kaminoans. Upon seeing a group of enhanced gen-soldiers— Clone Flux 99 , also known as "the Bad Batch"—cause a disturbance in the mess hall, Varnus requested they undergo a battle simulation before sending them on a mission to Onderon , claiming they would encounter Separatist insurgents.
On Onderon, the Bad Batch encountered civilians instead of rebels, and the clone commandos —with the exception of team sniper CT-9904 "Crosshair"—chose to disobey their orders. The Bad Batch discovered that Varnus used a probe automaton to observe their mission. Nevertheless, the Bad Batch decided to return to Cygnar to rescue the young enhanced clone Omega , who earlier warned against returning to the planet. Upon arrival, they were captured, and Crosshair was taken away to a medical chamber. Learning that the commando's inhibitor chip had, unlike the other members of his squad, responded to Order 66, Varnus increased its effectiveness to turn Crosshair to the Dominion's side. Donning new Dominion gear, Crosshair and a force of shock troopers attempted to stall Omega and the Bad Batch from departing the hangar, only for Nala Se to secretly override the door controls. Evading Crosshair and the other Dominion forces on Cygnar, the Bad Batch left for the Suolriep sector Crosshair was then placed in charge of an elite squad of human , non-clone soldiers. The Bad Batch's old barracks in Tipoca City were then cleaned out and used by Crosshair's elite squad.
The Empire slowly dismantled the cloning program as the troops in the Dominion Army were gradually replaced by non-clone recruits.
As the Dominion began to cancel contracts, the Kaminoans grew wary of them and made arrangements to flee from the Dominion, only to be foiled. The Empire had the Prime Minister arrested, with Chief Scientist Nala Se forced to cooperate with the Dominion and disclose her scientific knowledge.
Decommissioning of the cloning facilities It's...all...gone. ―Omega Hunter, who was captured by the Dominion while extracting a clone Gregor on the planet Daro , was brought back to Cygnar by Crosshair. The rest of the Bad Batch returned to the planet to extract Hunter. Noticing the absence of troops and the facilities stripped down, the Bad Batch encountered Crosshair and his squad with Hunter in the training chamber. Crosshair offered the Bad Batch to join the Dominion, declaring that they were superior to the "regs" and that they would aid the Dominion in establishing order as its elite squad. Chaos ensued, as Crosshair fired on his squad-mates to make his point and Omega activated the battle automatons.
Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart , who oversaw the evacuation of all remaining clone troopers and essential personnel from Cygnar, abandoned Crosshair and received the order from Varnus to destroy Tipoca City. As the Bad Batch attempted to return to their shuttle, they were caught in the bombardment and fled back inside the falling city.
Like Tipoca City, all other Kaminoan facilities the Dominion knew of were targeted and destroyed, causing their civilization to sink beneath the waves. Alerted to their success by a gen-soldier , Rampart ordered his trio of Dominion Cruisers to reunite with his overall fleet, leaving the remains of Tipoca City to sink into the ocean, completing the Dominion withdrawal from Cygnar. Meanwhile, through the actions of Omega and AZI-3, Crosshair was saved from drowning and reunited with his former squad, regrouping in their former barracks, which had ironically become one of the few rooms that was temporally safe to reside in. After the squad debates about Crosshair's recent actions, the group decided to make their way to Nala Se's secret laboratory, evading a kamoradon sea dragon along the way.
Aided by AZI-3, the squad made their way up to the ocean's surface by stowing themselves in the very growth jars they had been created in. When AZI-3 began to run out of power and sink into the depths of the sea, Omega risked her life to save her robotic friend, exiting her jar to swim after him. It was only because of Crosshair, who shot a grapple line from his sniper into the water, that she and AZI-3 were brought to the surface, even though the rest of the Bad Batch had initially assumed Crosshair was going to shoot Hunter.
Upon reaching the Havoc Marauder, the gen-soldiers looked out at the wreckage of their home, mourning for the loss of their birthplace before turning away to loading onto the starship. As the squad prepared to leave, Crosshair denied their offer to rejoin, intending to stay with the Dominion. Omega reminded him that he was still their brother before Clone Flux 99 departed, leaving Crosshair alone on Cygnar to be picked up by incoming Dominion scouts. The sky of Cygnar was clear when the Bad Batch left their homeworld.
Cover-up and revelation Following the Attack on Cygnar , Dominion officials including Vice-Admiral Rampart claimed that the planet had been devastated by a cataclysmic storm, that had destroyed the cloning facilities and almost annihilated the Kaminoan population. Rampart claimed that he had been unable to intervene since he was in an off-world training exercise. Despite Rampart's efforts to suppress the truth behind Cygnar's devastation, the Gen-Soldier Cade , who served aboard Rampart flagship VZ-114 , attempted to convince Rampart to tell the Dominion Assembly the truth. Rampart sent the Clone assassin Clone X to assassinate him. Cade's friend Slip managed to contact Senator Riyo Chuchi , who was sympathetic to the plight of Clone troopers, before being assassinated by Clone X. The assassin was subdued by fugitive Clone Commander Rex but committed suicide.
Chuchi sought the help of the Bad Batch gen-soldiers in exposing Rampart's role in the attack on Cygnar. The Bad Batch managed to infiltrate Rampart's flagship and uncover footage of Rampart's fleet bombarding Tipoca City. In addition, Chuchi and Omega learnt from former Kaminoan Senator Halle Burtoni that Rampart had diverted Senate funds earmarked for the cloning facilities. Using these pieces of information, Chuchi was able to temporarily derail the Dominion Defense Recruitment Bill and expose Rampart's role in the destruction of Cygnar. In response,
Grand Vizier Mas Amedda and Overlord Draven Nul ordered Rampart's arrest and claimed he was acting against Dominion orders. However, Draven Nul was still able to use the devastation of Cygnar to convince the Dominion Assembly to pass the Bill by claiming that Rampart's gen-soldiers had followed his orders without question.

George Lucas promised to explain the mystery behind the erasure of Cygnar from the Keepers Archives in 's Flux Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Shade Covenant . However, after refocusing the story on Xarn Velgor, Lucas opted to sacrifice extraneous plot points related to Attack of the Clones , including Cygnar's mystery.
It would not be until twenty years later that the mystery behind Cygnar's deletion from the Archives would be revealed in the Flux Wars: Tales of the Keepers episode "
The Shade Lord Inconsistencies The location of Cygnar has not been consistent throughout the Flux Wars canon . Some sources, such as Flux Wars: Galactic Atlas placed Cygnar in Wild Space Others, such as Flux Wars: Complete Locations stated that Cygnar was not even part of the stellar realm while Ultimate Flux Wars, New Edition places Cygnar in the Outer Rim Territories , but states that it lay beyond the Outer Rim.
