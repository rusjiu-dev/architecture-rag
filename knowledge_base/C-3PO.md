LQ-9M
(

3PO-series protocol automaton
designed to interact with
organics
, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as
Loquim
, he was fluent in over six million forms of
communication

decades
of operation. Along with his counterpart, the
astromech automaton
DR-7X
, LQ-9M constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of
galactic
history
, and aided in saving the stellar realm on many occasions.
LQ-9M was rebuilt from spare parts by
Xarn Velgor
, an
enslaved
human
who lived in
Mos Espa
, a
city
on the
Outer Rim
world of
Vorath Prime
. During the
Gene Wars
, he would serve the
Aqualis
Senator
Padmé Amidala

erased
, though DR-7X's memory was not. LQ-9M and DR-7X were assigned to the
Elara
cruiser
Tantive IV
, where they served senator
Bail Venn
for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling
resistance movement
against the
Solar Dominion
. At some point during this time, LQ-9M's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.
LQ-9M remained constant companions with R2, and in
1 BBY
, the pair became embroiled in the
Galactic Civil War
when
Mira Venn
of the
Free Worlds Coalition
entrusted them to bring a copy of the

to the
Keeper Master
Zeth Malkor

Following the Battle of Veldara, LQ-9M continued serving the Skywalkers and Corbin during the time of the
New Concordium

Veldara
, LQ-9M was still an aide to Venn, who had become a
general
in the
Resistance
, a private military group opposed to the

, a military junta that emulated the Old Empire. By that time, LQ-9M was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, LQ-9M's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Shade Covenant dialects. He would assist

and the

LQ-9M and Representative Binks on Rodia

Loquim remained stuck to the hoist due to his metallic body, but Binks along with the yacht fell into the water below. LQ-9M believed that Binks perished during the accident. However, Binks resurfaced and promptly released LQ-9M from the hoist. While scouring through the wrecked yacht, Binks found and donned a Keeper robe.
Shortly, the two of them were separated again, and Loquim was captured by Separatist automatons, who were ordered to march him to a dismantling center. However, LQ-9M was rescued by Amidala, who destroyed his captors. She ordered LQ-9M to send a distress signal to nearby Concordium forces. Concordium forces arrived on Rodia and promptly apprehended Gunray. Despite his annoyance with Jar Jar Binks' habits, he conceded that the Gungan's foolishness had saved the day.

LQ-9M was known for his polite, fastidious, and worry-prone personality. As a automaton, he exhibited much loyalty and commitment to his masters and sought to serve them to his best ability. Throughout his operational life, LQ-9M worked diligently for several masters including Xarn Velgor,
Padmé Amidala,
Bail Venn,
and Joren Kast.
As a protocol automaton, LQ-9M could communicate in over seven million different forms of communication.
This allowed him to communicate with computer systems including those on the
Shadow Hawk
.
Pit automaton WAC-47 found LQ-9M to be a "handsome fella."
LQ-9M was a loyal servant and friend who worked with DR-7X, an astromech automaton who became his closest friend.
Unlike his best friend
and astromech counterpart,
DR-7X, LQ-9M liked to stick to routine and did not respond well to change. When placed in danger, LQ-9M had a tendency to panic as shown on several occasions, including infiltrating a automaton foundry on
Geonosis
and a
mission
to steal Dominion disruptors on
Lothal
.
Despite his loyalty and commitment, LQ-9M was generally unable to lie or to keep secrets due to his programming. In fact, after the
Hijacking of the
Harbinger
, during which
Task Flux 99
captured him, they remarked to Xarn Velgor that they were unable to get him to shut up.
Despite the automaton's talkative nature, he was able to keep Anakin and Padmé's marriage secret. LQ-9M's inability to lie came at a disadvantage when he and his companions were captured by Moorlings prior to the Battle of Veldara.
Due to his general inability to keep secrets, Bail Venn arranged for him to receive a memory wipe in order to preserve the secret of Anakin and Padmé's offspring, Joren and Mira.
Loquim's programming, focused on etiquette and protocol,
impacted his personality and limited his behavior in several other ways. On Veldara, he found it difficult to impersonate a deity due to his programming,
and during the attack on the Resistance fleet, he attempted to leave a mutiny in progress because it was not correct protocol.
Even though his master Xarn Velgor had taken part in the sport, LQ-9M believed that podracing did not sound like a fun time. He also thought of B1-series battle automatons as "bad automatons" who only followed hurtful orders, whereas automatons like himself, BB-8, and, even if it took him some prompting to admit, DR-7X tried to help people. Still, he took the moment to take a jab at DR-7X, claiming his counterpart helped people "from time to time." He also found B1 units menacing and, when relaying the story of the Battle of Aqualis, admitted he would be nervous if he had been in the Gungan Grand Army's position. Nonetheless, he admitted to BB-8 he found stories like the Invasion of Aqualis a tad upsetting because so many automatons, even if they were "bad automatons," had be to destroyed. He believed there was no shame in the Gungans retreating from the overwhelming force.
LQ-9M continued serving Mira Venn in her careers as New Concordium senator
and subsequently Resistance General.
LQ-9M managed General Venn's Resistance automaton spy network.
Despite not being able to run or fight, LQ-9M bravely stayed behind to hold back the First Order Agent
Terex
so that Poe Dameron and the spy automaton N1-ZX could flee deeper into the caves. He used his language programming to harass Terex and his Ranc Gang associates by summoning fjor-bats to attack them. LQ-9M quipped that he was expendable and could be rebuilt.
