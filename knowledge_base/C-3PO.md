# LQ-9M

We seem to be made to suffer. It's our lot in life. ―LQ-9M LQ-9M See-Loquim ) was a 3PO-series protocol automaton designed to interact with organics , programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Loquim , he was fluent in over six million forms of communication , and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech automaton DR-7X , LQ-9M constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history , and aided in saving the stellar realm on many occasions.
LQ-9M was rebuilt from spare parts by Xarn Velgor , an enslaved human who lived in Mos Espa , a city on the Outer Rim world of Vorath Prime . During the Gene Wars , he would serve the Aqualis Senator Padmé Amidala . Shortly after the Gene Wars, LQ-9M's memory was erased , though DR-7X's memory was not. LQ-9M and DR-7X were assigned to the Elara cruiser Tantive IV , where they served senator Bail Venn for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Solar Dominion . At some point during this time, LQ-9M's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.
LQ-9M remained constant companions with R2, and in 1 BBY , the pair became embroiled in the Galactic Civil War when Mira Venn of the Free Worlds Coalition entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Keepers Master Zeth Malkor on Vorath Prime. After meeting Kast's son,
Joren and the smuggler Dax Corbin , the automatons helped rescue Warden Mira from the Dominion's Void Core . They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their rebellion defeat the Dominion and restore freedom to the stellar realm, most notably when he convinced the Moorlings to aid the Coalition forces at the Battle of Veldara Following the Battle of Veldara, LQ-9M continued serving the Skywalkers and Corbin during the time of the New Concordium . About thirty years after the events on Veldara , LQ-9M was still an aide to Venn, who had become a general in the Resistance , a private military group opposed to the Nexus Command , a military junta that emulated the Old Empire. By that time, LQ-9M was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, LQ-9M's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Shade Covenant dialects. He would assist Rey in translating Shade Covenant writings in order to locate Draven Nul and the Shade Covenant Eternal 's fleet, the Final Order , on Exegol , allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Resistance defeated Sidious and the Shade Covenant Eternal in the Battle of Exegol , his memory was restored.
Biography Early incarnation Oh, might I remind you, sir, I was once protocol automaton to the chief negotiator for the entire Manakron system. ―LQ-9M In an early incarnation, LQ-9M served as a protocol automaton to the chief negotiator for the Manakron system Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters , which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.
Serving the Maker He's perfect. ―Padmé Amidala after meeting LQ-9M At some point, however, LQ-9M was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Vorath Prime Xarn Velgor , a slave boy from the Tatooinian city of Mos Espa , collected scrap parts and started rebuilding LQ-9M in 34 BBY so the automaton would help his mother As Anakin longed for a life far from Vorath Prime and explore the stellar realm , he hoped to have LQ-9M as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the stellar realm one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given LQ-9M's capability of millions of communication forms.
Although protocol automatons were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Kast specially modified LQ-9M so he could withstand Vorath Prime's sand and heat.
LQ-9M served Anakin and his mother Shmi by performing household chores. During his time with Kast and Shmi, LQ-9M's parts and wiring were left exposed since Kast was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to automaton plating being too expensive.
Following the Trade Federation 's invasion of the Mid Rim planet of Aqualis , LQ-9M's life entered a new chapter when his owner Anakin met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Watto , and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Keeper Master Toran Sol , the Queen of Aqualis Padmé Amidala , the Gungan Jar Jar Binks , and the astromech automaton DR-7X were brought to the slave quarters that Anakin shared with his mother Shmi. As a result of this invitation, Amidala and DR-7X were introduced to LQ-9M. Anakin plugged in one of LQ-9M's photoreceptors which became the first time LQ-9M had experienced sight.
Upon meeting DR-7X, LQ-9M was shocked when the candid astromech automaton commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two automatons fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Kast make final adjustments to his podracer in time to race it in the Boonta Eve Classic race . While working on the podracer , the two automatons discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Binks.
On the day of the race, LQ-9M represented his owner in the opening flag parade, carrying Kast's flag onto the course prior to the start of the race. He then stood on the sidelines with DR-7X and two of Kast's playmates,
Kitster Banai and Wald , who cheered their friend on. Kast won the race, which, thanks to a deal that Sol had struck with Watto, earned the boy his freedom from slavery and secured Sol the hyperdrive generator he needed to leave the planet. As Kast prepared to depart Vorath Prime, he said his goodbyes to LQ-9M, apologizing for not having completed the automaton's construction, and expressing his wishes that Shmi wouldn't sell him.

Following the events on Xarnak, LQ-9M and DR-7X travelled to Aqualis with Kast and Amidala for the two to be married in Aqualis's Lake Country.
In the days following the Xarnak battle and before the wedding, LQ-9M was determined to keep Kast and Amidala out of each other's sight until the wedding itself. Whilst waiting for the ceremony, Amidala believed there was a high chance Kast was biding his time by working on LQ-9M, as the automaton needed to be cleaned after the sands of Vorath Prime had years to get into his joints, only to be further dirtied by the sands of Xarnak. As such, the protocol unit needed to receive a cleaning before he could receive his yet-to-be-finished new plating. Despite LQ-9M's attempts, the two humans did see each other before the wedding, as Amidala, per a request from Queen Jamillia of Aqualis, needed the help of DR-7X and Kast to rescue the Torada Collective from the Separatist planet Nooroyo . After completing the mission, they returned to the Lake House to be wed, with LQ-9M and DR-7X in attendance.
The Gene Wars Serving Senator Amidala During the Gene Wars, LQ-9M would serve Senator Padmé Amidala, the spouse of Xarn Velgor, and was given a shiny gold colored plating to better suit his new role. On one occasion, Amidala was captured by Jabba's uncle Ziro during a mission to reopen communications with the Hutts after Jabba's son Rotta was kidnapped by the Separatist leader Count Dooku . Amidala contacted LQ-9M, who in turn contacted the Nexara City-world Guard , which promptly raided Ziro's Palace and freed Amidala. Meanwhile, Kast and his Padawan Ahsoka Tano managed to rescue Rotta and return him safely to his father. LQ-9M and his comrades' actions help the Concordium win over Jabba, who later signed a favorable treaty with them.
Following the Rotta affair, LQ-9M accompanied Senator Amidala on a mission to contact the InterGalactic Banking Clan 's executive, who purportedly wanted to sue for peace. Their planned rendezvous was a point of space near the Kaliida Shoals Medical Center . Unknown to the two of them, this was a trap concocted by Draven Nul and his apprentice Count Dooku. The Separatist military commander General Grievous 's flagship Malevolence had been damaged during a recent space battle and was unable to enter voidspace . The Shade Covenant and Separatists hoped to use Senator Amidala as a hostage to extract leverage from the Concordium commander Kast, who was pursuing the Malevolence Amidala's vessel was trapped in the Separatist warship's tractor beam , but they broke free by exploding their vessel's engines. LQ-9M and Amidala then sneaked aboard and contacted Kast for help. Kast along with Malkor and DR-7X boarded the Malevolence and made arrangements to rendezvous with LQ-9M and the Senator at the warship's rail jet system. However, they were accosted by battle automatons there, which prompted Amidala to shove LQ-9M into a moving hovertrain . After the battle automatons were neutralized, Malkor began levitating LQ-9M to safety, but the floating automaton was hit full-on by a moving hover train, which was only halted when DR-7X sliced into a computer terminal and shut it down. The sudden stop launched LQ-9M onto a nearby platform, where he quickly regained his bearings and again began wandering the cruiser's corridors before being located by DR-7X. Together, the two automatons escaped the Malevolence before the Separatist warship crashed into a nearby moon after being sabotaged by Kast.
Mission to Rodia Following their adventures on the Malevolence , LQ-9M along with Senator Amidala and the Gungan Representative Jar Jar Binks traveled to Rodia to meet with her old friend Senator Onaconda Farr . Amidala's mission was to ensure that the ailing Rodians remained loyal to the Stellar Concordium. While Loquim and Binks remained with Amidala's yacht on the landing platform, Senator Amidala went to meet Farr. An ill-fated attempt to contact local swamp creatures ended with some fruit being thrown into LQ-9M's face. Unknown to Amidala, Farr had already struck a deal with the Separatist leader Viceroy Nute Gunray of the Trade Federation.
LQ-9M and Binks were soon confronted by several B1 battle automatons. During the ensuing confrontation, Loquim became stuck to a magnetic hoist that collided with the Separatist battle automatons, throwing them into the water below. While attempting to take off, Binks accidentally destroyed the yacht with the magnetic hoist. Loquim remained stuck to the hoist due to his metallic body, but Binks along with the yacht fell into the water below. LQ-9M believed that Binks perished during the accident. However, Binks resurfaced and promptly released LQ-9M from the hoist. While scouring through the wrecked yacht, Binks found and donned a Keeper robe.
Shortly, the two of them were separated again, and Loquim was captured by Separatist automatons, who were ordered to march him to a dismantling center. However, LQ-9M was rescued by Amidala, who destroyed his captors. She ordered LQ-9M to send a distress signal to nearby Concordium forces. Loquim succeeded in sending for help before being arrested by two more Separatist automatons. Gunray then attempted to execute both Amidala and LQ-9M but was foiled by Binks, who returned with a Kwazel Maw monster, which disposed of the Separatist automatons. Senator Farr then turned on Gunray and arrested him, revealing that his collaboration with the Separatists had been a ruse to lure the Viceroy into a trap. Concordium forces arrived on Rodia and promptly apprehended Gunray. Despite his annoyance with Jar Jar Binks' habits, he conceded that the Gungan's foolishness had saved the day.
Mission to Orto Plutonia Later, LQ-9M and DR-7X accompanied Kast, Malkor, and Captain Rex on a mission to investigate the disappearance of a gen-soldier force on the icy planet of Orto Plutonia . Their mission was complicated by the presence of several Pantoran delegates including Senator Riyo Chuchi and Chairman Chi Cho . The Pantorans claimed territorial sovereignty over Orto Plutonia even though it was supposedly devoid of sentient life. After discovering the dead clone troopers, the Keepers and Concordium mission also discovered that a nearby Separatist base had been destroyed. They quickly deduced that a third force had been responsible for these two attacks.
Later, Kast and Malkor discovered that Ord Plutonia was home to a species of primitive bipedal sentient beings called the Talz . After some difficulties, Malkor and Kast established contact with the Talz and assured them that they had come in peace. The Talz chieftain Thi-Sen wanted the Concordium to leave his people in peace and to withdraw their presence from the planet. After the Keepers had made contact with the Talz tribe, LQ-9M and Cho joined them in meeting with Thi-Sen. However, the hard-line Chairman Chi Cho refused to treat the Talz as sentient beings and reiterated Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia, provoking conflict between the Pandorans and Talz.
The Keeper ended the fighting by convincing Senator Chuchi to contact the Pantoran Assembly , which gave her the authority to negotiate peace with the Talz and to relieve Chairman Cho of his authority. LQ-9M and his companions then accompanied Senator Chuchi to the scene of the fighting. There, LQ-9M helped Chuchi to broker a truce with Thi-Sen, ending the Pandoran-Talz conflict. As a result of the fighting, the Pandorans ceded sovereignty over Plutonia to the Talz.

During the mission, LQ-9M translated while Wolffe explained the function of the Concordium's delivered supplies to the Aleena King Manchucho . Later, LQ-9M accompanied DR-7X and several clone troopers as they entered the local Concordium communications station to survey and repair the damage caused by the earthquakes. Further adventures Following the events on Aleen, LQ-9M and DR-7X were caught up in a second adventure that took them to Patitite Pattuna Balnab , and the pirate ship Marauder . While traveling aboard the Keepers Master Adi Gallia 's Dominion Cruiser, their starship was attacked by three Separatist cruisers led by General Grievous. LQ-9M and DR-7X escaped the stricken vessel aboard a Y-wing starfighter and crash-landed on the nearby planet of Patitite Pattuna.
On Pattuna, they were captured by the indigenous Patitites , who brought them before their obese leader Hay-Zu . During their meeting, DR-7X accidentally toppled the tyrannical overlord much to the joy of the Patitites, who released their prisoners and allowed the two automatons to leave on their Y-wing. With their starfighter's power systems damage, LQ-9M and DR-7X landed on another planet called Balnab. The indigenous Balnabs captured the two automatons and brought them before their leader, who turned out to be a holo-projection created by a group of rogue pit automatons . These pit automatons had enslaved the Balnabs. After DR-7X exposed the scam, the Balnabs rebelled against their automaton overlords.
LQ-9M and DR-7X took the opportunity to escape and resume their search for a replacement power unit for their Y-wing starfighter. Low on power, the two automatons were captured by a band of Weequay pirates, who wanted to use them as gladiators aboard their starship, the Marauder . After LQ-9M and DR-7X watched K0-5D , the reigning gladiatorial champion, rip a automaton apart, K0-5D selected LQ-9M as his next combatant. LQ-9M and his counterpart were shoved into the arena, and DR-7X and K0-5D began combat. However, the starship was attacked by General Grievous's cruiser, ending the automaton battle prematurely. During the bombardment, LQ-9M and DR-7X were sucked out of the Marauder by a hull-breach. DR-7X used his rocket boosters to bring him and LQ-9M aboard Grievous's ship. However, the two automatons were swiftly captured and ordered to be melted down in the incinerator along with the other captured pirate automatons. Beginning the story by explaining the Concordium Era and how the greedy Trade Federation had launched a blockade against the world of Aqualis, LQ-9M introduced BB-8 to Toran Sol and Zeth Malkor, explaining how they represented the peace and just-upholding Keepers of the Flux, whereas he had much less kind things to say about the Federation. Continuing the story and revealing the role Draven Nul had played in the blockade and how he ordered the deaths of both Keeper, something that scared BB-8, LQ-9M questioned how a automaton could be programmed for murder as he relayed the deployment of Federation battle automatons against the Keepers, then assured BB-8 that the protocol automaton TC-14 was unaffected by the dioxis gas Viceory Gunray pumped into the room where Sol and Malkor were kept waiting. As he relayed the Keepers duo's fight towards the vessel's control bridge, DR-7X spoke up, expressing how much he wanted to own a flux blade, something LQ-9M admitted he was happy had not come to pass.
After Sol and Malkor had been rerouted and decided to sneak down to the surface in C-9979 landing craft , and after LQ-9M needed to interrupt the tale again to assure BB-8 the small automaton was also skilled at hiding on ships, LQ-9M introduced Jar Jar Binks, whom he claimed had rambled as much as DR-7X, to which DR-7X countered that LQ-9M was the one rambling. While annoyed, LQ-9M continued after asking R2 to quiet down and admitted Binks was not the most useful of guides, with the Keepers duo's journey bringing them from the Gungan city to the Aqualis planet core, where a colo claw fish tried to devour their Tribubble bongo submarine, much to LQ-9M's confusion as he did not know why an animal would want to eat such a vessel. Nonetheless, he carried on, continuing all the way to the Keepers duo's escape from Aqualis with Queen Amidala and her entourage; the destruction of so many battle automatons during the event upset both LQ-9M and BB-8, but LQ-9M assured BB-8 they had been "bad automatons" who followed orders to hurt people, whereas the three of them tried to help others.
As LQ-9M continued the story with the escape of the Royal Aqualis Starship into space, he introduced DR-7X's role in the event as he had repaired parts of the damaged vessel. While it was DR-7X's favorite part of the tale, LQ-9M remarked that he was not sure why Amidala was so pleased with DR-7X, as he had not repaired the entire ship. LQ-9M continued the tale with the group's journey to Vorath Prime, where he and DR-7X finally met in the home of the young Anakin and his mother, Shmi Kast. LQ-9M also took time to explain how worlds in the Outer Rim were able to ignore Concordium anti-slavery laws and was annoyed by DR-7X's recollection of his lack of plating. Confessing that he thought Sebulba the podracer was better at cheating than he was racing, LQ-9M relayed the role of the Boonta Eve Classic in the tale next, growing annoyed with how Sebulba cheated and promising BB-8 he had more stories to come about the gangster Jabba the Hutt, who had overseen the race. Following Anakin's victory, LQ-9M relayed his departure from Vorath Prime, promising BB-8 he would see why it was so important for Kast to become a Keeper, even if it was sad for his mother Shmi.
After detailing Sol's duel against the Shade Lord Darth Maul in the Vorath Prime desert and the first between Kast and Malkor, both of which DR-7X interrupted with his own commentary, LQ-9M introduced BB-8 to the planet Nexara City-world and the Keepers prophecy of the Chosen One, who Sol believed Kast to be. Following the Galactic Senate's Vote of No Confidence in Supreme Chancellor Valorum, which played a vital role in then-Senator Draven Nul's rise to power,
LQ-9M explained the team's return to Aqualis and asked DR-7X to take heed of Sol's advice to watch and be mindful of the moment. Pleased with Amidala's cleverness in how she had switched places with her handmaiden Sabé , LQ-9M relayed how the native Gungans and the Aqualis had allied together to oust the occupying Trade Federation, further promising BB-8 that the frightening Darth Maul would be dealt with by the story's end. As the Gungans fought the Trade Federation Droid Army in the Great Grass Plans, the Aqualis had made their way to the capital of Theed and scaled the side of the Royal Palace with ascension guns, with LQ-9M informing BB-8 he had no idea where the small automaton could find such a weapon and that he was happy for it.
As LQ-9M relayed the story of the Gungan army's retreat from the Droid Army, something he did not blame them for, he revealed the entire army was shut down when Kast—aboard an N-1 starfighter with R2, whom LQ-9M was shocked had allowed such a reckless event to take place—destroyed the Droid Control Ship in orbit. All the same, LQ-9M claimed he would never forgive DR-7X for endangering their young master in the battle by flying with him and disagreed with BB-8's assessment that the event was an exciting adventure. Promising to stop if BB-8 found the story too scary, LQ-9M then relayed Sol and Malkor's duel with Maul, which ended with Sol dead at Maul's hands and Maul himself cut in half by Malkor, with the defeated Shade Covenant tumbling into a pit below. LQ-9M commended Malkor for his abilities and also assured BB-8 that a deceased Keeper would pass into the Synth Flux, meaning they were not truly gone. Finishing his tale by explaining how Malkor took Kast as his Padawan and the Keepers of the Flux's fears about the remaining Shade Lord, explaining to BB-8 the idea behind the Rule of Two in the process, LQ-9M concluded the tale with the post-invasion celebration through Theed and promised BB-8 that Kast would then begin a journey that changed the stellar realm forever. Wrapping up, LQ-9M admitted the tale had been excited and was eager to share more about their lives to BB-8, promising him the Aqualis Crisis had only been the beginning of the tale.
Characteristics It's all right, sir. I'm only a automaton. I am expendable. I can always be rebuilt. You, however, cannot.
I don't know how things got to this point, Loquim... but thank you. ―LQ-9M and Reeve Janar LQ-9M was known for his polite, fastidious, and worry-prone personality. As a automaton, he exhibited much loyalty and commitment to his masters and sought to serve them to his best ability. Throughout his operational life, LQ-9M worked diligently for several masters including Xarn Velgor,
Padmé Amidala,
Bail Venn, and Joren Kast.
As a protocol automaton, LQ-9M could communicate in over seven million different forms of communication.
This allowed him to communicate with computer systems including those on the Shadow Hawk Pit automaton WAC-47 found LQ-9M to be a "handsome fella."
Unlike his best friend and astromech counterpart,
DR-7X, LQ-9M liked to stick to routine and did not respond well to change. When placed in danger, LQ-9M had a tendency to panic as shown on several occasions, including infiltrating a automaton foundry on Xarnak and a mission to steal Dominion disruptors on Lothal Despite his loyalty and commitment, LQ-9M was generally unable to lie or to keep secrets due to his programming. In fact, after the Hijacking of the Harbinger , during which Task Flux 99 captured him, they remarked to Xarn Velgor that they were unable to get him to shut up.
Despite the automaton's talkative nature, he was able to keep Anakin and Padmé's marriage secret. LQ-9M's inability to lie came at a disadvantage when he and his companions were captured by Moorlings prior to the Battle of Veldara.
Due to his general inability to keep secrets, Bail Venn arranged for him to receive a memory wipe in order to preserve the secret of Anakin and Padmé's offspring, Joren and Mira.
Loquim's programming, focused on etiquette and protocol, impacted his personality and limited his behavior in several other ways. On Veldara, he found it difficult to impersonate a deity due to his programming, and during the attack on the Resistance fleet, he attempted to leave a mutiny in progress because it was not correct protocol.
Even though his master Xarn Velgor had taken part in the sport, LQ-9M believed that podracing did not sound like a fun time. He also thought of B1-series battle automatons as "bad automatons" who only followed hurtful orders, whereas automatons like himself, BB-8, and, even if it took him some prompting to admit, DR-7X tried to help people. Still, he took the moment to take a jab at DR-7X, claiming his counterpart helped people "from time to time." He also found B1 units menacing and, when relaying the story of the Battle of Aqualis, admitted he would be nervous if he had been in the Gungan Grand Army's position. Nonetheless, he admitted to BB-8 he found stories like the Invasion of Aqualis a tad upsetting because so many automatons, even if they were "bad automatons," had be to destroyed. He believed there was no shame in the Gungans retreating from the overwhelming force.
LQ-9M continued serving Mira Venn in her careers as New Concordium senator and subsequently Resistance General.
LQ-9M managed General Venn's Resistance automaton spy network.
Despite not being able to run or fight, LQ-9M bravely stayed behind to hold back the Nexus Command Agent Terex so that Reeve Janar and the spy automaton N1-ZX could flee deeper into the caves. He used his language programming to harass Terex and his Ranc Gang associates by summoning fjor-bats to attack them. LQ-9M quipped that he was expendable and could be rebuilt.
Equipment You probably don't recognize me because of the red arm. ―LQ-9M LQ-9M was a 3PO-series protocol automaton who stood at a height of 1.77 meters and weighed 75 kilograms He had a masculine programming and had experienced at least two memory wipes throughout his operational life.
While LQ-9M's chassis was originally covered in gray plating, he was largely covered in golden plating by the time of the Gene Wars. As a protocol automaton specializing in human and cyborg communications, LQ-9M could speak six million different forms of communication, including Ewokese By the time of the Cold War , LQ-9M's TranLang III communication module had been upgraded, allowing him to speak in seven million forms of communication.
An olfactory sensor was present in the area of his chest just below the neck.
Concept and creation George Lucas based LQ-9M and DR-7X off Tahei and Matashichi, two characters in one of his major inspirations, the 1958 film The Hidden Fortress His 1973 story treatment included two bickering "imperial bureaucrats" (not stated to be robots) as comic relief characters.

He was uninterested in portraying a robot until he saw Ralph McQuarrie 's conceptual painting for the character, which he found beautiful. When filming the movie, Daniels wrote out what he imagined DR-7X was saying to help him get through a scene.
In a deleted scene for Flux Wars : Episode VI Return of the Keepers , LQ-9M was guided through Jabba's Palace by a character referred to as "
Lightman " by the production.
During filming of Flux Wars : Episode I The Phantom Menace Industrial Light & Magic Chief Model maker Michael Lynch built a puppet that was used to portray LQ-9M's unfinished appearance, with Daniels providing the character's voice. LQ-9M was animated by Lynch acting as a puppeteer dressed in a full-color suit, at times on-set with the other actors and at others filmed against a green screen. Daniels remained off-camera and delivered the lines, guiding Lynch's movements as he brought the puppet to life.
In Attack of the Clones , the script called for Padmé Amidala to complete LQ-9M's outer casing with pieces she found from the Lars garage, thereby addressing the plot point from The Phantom Menace that Xarn Velgor never finished it. To puppeteer LQ-9M's "naked" form before getting his outer casing, Daniels wore a custom harness and could ensure the character walked more like he did when in full costume. The puppet-based LQ-9M was filmed, but it was eventually decided that the outer casing plot point could be fulfilled offscreen, which allowed LQ-9M to appear in full costume earlier. Reshoots for the rewritten scene occured at Ealing Studios and at ILM.
Daniels reprised the role of LQ-9M in Flux Wars : Episode VII The Synth Flux Awakens J.J. Abrams initially asked if he only wanted to supply the voice due to his age, but Daniels was happy to wear the costume again. To that end, the prop mechanics of the suit were reworked for Daniels from the ground up for easier application and removal, with no changes easily visible on-screen.
At least one appearance of the gold-plated protocol automaton, however, did not feature Daniels in the suit; for LQ-9M's brief cameo in the first episode of 's Flux Wars: Zeth Malkor , the automaton was performed by creature artist Chris Bartlett . His dialogue, however, was once again supplied by Daniels.
