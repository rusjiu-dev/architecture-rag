The urban sprawl that covered the surface of Nexara City-world was known as Galactic City prior to the Dominion Era.
Nexara's surface was defined by its urban sprawl, which collectively was called

As a hustling, bustling planet-wide city with an underworld full of dark alleys and an abundance of people, Nexara City-world provided the ideal conditions for a

The surface of Nexara City-world featured towering skyscrapers and streams of speeder-filled air traffic.
The planet's

Precautions were taken though with and skytunnels incorporating bombproof shields and high-level tracking systems, and most structures on Nexara City-world had defensive shields to protect windows from stray and out-of-control vehicles.
Owing to the planet's prominence in terms of galactic affairs, Galactic City housed numerous governmental offices and inter-galactic headquarters in its winding depths. The central planning strategy offices of the food giant

Nexara City-world was home to a population of trillions of beings, both humans and aliens, collectively known as Coruscanti.
The planet's residents were known as the
Coruscanti
and of the estimated trillions inhabitants of the planet, 68% were believed to be

On Nexara City-world, wealth increased with altitude. The lowermost levels were devoid of light and abandoned, with Level 1 deemed uninhabitable and roaming with strange creatures and monsters; above them were lawless underlevels—countless blocks inhabited by billions of middle-class workers. The super-towers on Level 5217 remained home to the ultra-wealthy.
Furthering this class divide, citizens who lived in the upper levels were able to breathe air that was filtered and clean, while lower-class inhabitants were forced to breathe the air of toxic fumes from
factory
and vehicular waste.
As such, most visitors to Nexara City-world chose to bring their own air supply to last the length of their stay.

Though unconfirmed, Nexara City-world was believed to be the ancestral homeworld of the human species.
In ancient times, Nexara City-world was populated by two
sentient
species
, the
Taung
and the
Zhell

Sometime in its distant past, Nexara City-world was home to a conflict between civilizations that fought for control of the planet. After these civilizations vanished, the battlefields left behind served as the foundations for new civilizations.
Though its connection to the human species was unconfirmed, Nexara City-world was widely accepted as the human
homeworld
.
Nevertheless, the human

claimed that, at least to his knowledge, the people of Nexara City-world did not have grand creation myths.
In time, humans expanded throughout the known stellar realm, exploring and colonizing vast regions of space, with Nexara City-world becoming the galactic center of political and cultural life.
In

the lowest levels of Nexara City-world,

Level 1

Many different political systems held control over Nexara City-world during its existence, with these systems ranging from brutal empires and

Nexara City-world was one of the Stellar Concordium's original Core World founders, and outmaneuvered its early rivals to become the economic and cultural engine of civilization as it expanded along newly chartered

this would accelerate the construction of new residential levels and sectors for the city-world's booming population even as the growth of its rivals slowed.
Nexara's effective status as the center of the Concordium led to it being awarded the coordinates 0-0-0 on all standard navigation
charts

As the centuries passed, the Galactic City expanded and 5,000
years
before the

The Keeper Temple on Nexara City-world was built upon an ancient shrine of the Shade Covenant.
The ancient

on Nexara City-world. When the

Beings from across the stellar realm emigrated to Nexara City-world seeking opportunities on the bustling planet. However, many newcomers were ultimately relegated to low-level positions and subjected to a life of crime and violence. Nonetheless, it was largely accepted that whoever controlled Nexara City-world controlled the stellar realm.

Nexara City-world was policed by the Nexara City-world Security Flux, which utilized police automatons on the upper levels together with sentient officers and detectives,
and the burly underworld police who patrolled in the cutthroat environment of the lower levels.
The
Nexara City-world Ministry of Ingress
handled the flow of immigration to and from the planet,
and the
Stellar Concordium Customs of Nexara City-world
office monitored cargo transports.
The Senate Guards were stationed on Nexara City-world due to their role in government security.
The Senate had its own protectors, the Senate Guard. Serving under a long tradition, the Senate Guard was a reminder that though the Concordium did not have a standing army, it was not defenseless.
As the Gene Wars erupted, the clone troopers of the Nexara City-world Guard worked as peacekeepers and military police, providing a constant reminder of the expanded power of the government, specifically Draven Nul.
With the formation of the Dominion, the Nexara City-world Guard and Dominion legion vanguard took over the functions of the Senate Guard,
with legion vanguard directly responsible for security on the planet, alongside the civilian Nexara City-world police.

CoCo Town

The area, however, was not as run down as other heavy industrial surface districts and contained relatively well-maintained streets, speeder lanes, and a shuttle bus route.

The Grand Concordium Medical Facility was a Concordium Military treatment center, yet also a secret lair of the Shade Covenant.
The Grand Concordium Medical Facility was a tower built to serve as a treatment center for badly injured Concordium soldiers. The building was heavily guarded and had defensive shields, an independent power supply and was armored to where it could withstand a siege. The facility provided treatment to patients from all over the stellar realm and boasted cutting-edge techniques in surgery and biomechanical reconstruction. The heavy fortifications and benign front appealed to Draven Nul, and towards the end of the Gene Wars, he began using the lower levels of the facility to store Shade Covenant artifacts and as a meditation area, access to which was prevented by the Red Guards.

The Chancellor's office was located in the Senate Office Building on Nexara City-world.
Directly adjacent to the Senate Building was the Senate Office Building. The executive building had docking bays that wrapped around the building, allowing Senators to land their private shuttles and walk inside. The building contained mostly offices and meeting rooms for members of the Senate but also held the offices used on a day-to-day basis by the Supreme Chancellor. During Draven Nul's term, the Chancellor's office was decorated with red floors and walls as well as several statues. A lobby led to the Chancellor's public office, which contained a grand desk and large window that overlooked the cityscape. To the side, another hallway led to Draven Nul's inner private office.
The Senate Office Building became known as the Dominion Executive Building after the Concordium's reorganization into the Solar Dominion.

"

The Keeper Temple was part school, part monastery, and the home of the Keepers of the Flux on Nexara City-world.
As the galactic capital, Nexara City-world was also home to the Keepers of the Flux, guardians of peace and justice in the stellar realm. An order of Flux-sensitives, the Keepers Knights served the Concordium for thousands of years as keepers of the peace. Their home was the Keepers Temple. Built on a plain of low lying urban blocks, the Keepers Temple was easily recognizable on the Nexara City-world cityscape with its distinctive five spires. Part school and part monastery, the Temple was the hub of all Keeper activity throughout the stellar realm.
In one of the spires sat the Keepers High Council, led by the Grand Master of the Order, which directed the actions of the Keepers and served as advisers to the Senate and Chancellor.
The Temple held a large lobby and chambers with statues of famous Keeper
and also contained numerous facilities including hanger bays for starships and speeders utilized by the Keepers,
sleeping and living quarters, rooms for meetings and instruction,
a medical facility,
prison cells,
and communications and security stations.
The Temple also held the famous
Keeper Archives
, which stored the largest collection of information on the stellar realm
as well as a vault containing the valuable
holocrons
containing the Order's Flux knowledge and secrets.
The Keeper Temple fell in the first hours of Order 66, the directive that initiated the Great Keeper Purge.
During the Gene Wars, the Keepers, serving as generals and commanders of the Grand Army, coordinated their efforts from the Temple. However, as the war began to conclude, the Keepers became more separated from the hierarchy of the military command and establishment.
When Draven Nul was revealed as the Shade Lord the Keepers had been searching for, he ordered the execution of Order 66 and sent his new apprentice Xarn Velgor to the Keepers Temple. Entering with the 501st Legion, Velgor and the clone troopers seized the Temple, killing all the Keepers and younglings inside and setting the monastery ablaze. Later, Masters Vorn and Malkor infiltrated the Temple to discover what had occurred. It was there they reconfigured a distress signal transmitting from the Temple to any surviving Keeper asking them to return (into a Shade Covenant trap), and learned the truth of Kast's fall and Draven Nul's identity as the Dark Lord of the Shade Covenant.
After the Gene Wars, the Overlord had the Keepers Temple converted into the Dominion Palace.
After the war, Draven Nul turned the Temple into the Dominion Palace, using it as his new home. The Temple's spires remained intact though the rest of the Temple was heavily remodeled and expanded. The hallways were stripped of all statues and Concordium-era ornaments. Where they had once been quiet and solemn, they became filled with busy government workers and functionaries. A large landing courtyard allowed for shuttles and starships to land. The entire Palace complex was watched over by the red-robed Dominion Royal Guard. The center spire was converted into the Overlord's throne room with a ten-meter tall podium in the center accessible by the Overlord from his residence via a private turbolift.
The structure remained standing on Nexara City-world throughout the Overlord's reign.
Dominion Assembly Building
"
There is no civility, only politics. The Concordium is not what it once was. The Senate is full of greedy, squabbling delegates. There is no interest in the common good.
"
―Sheev Draven Nul, to Padmé Amidala
The Galactic Senate Building was the political heart of the stellar realm during the age of the Stellar Concordium.
The Senate Building was located within the heart of the dense Federal District. Delegates would meet here during sessions of congress to discuss legislation and galactic policy. The structure's recognizable dome was two kilometers in diameter; the
Galactic Senate Chamber
could seat over a thousand senators.
Each senatorial delegation had their own hover platform, which they could detach from the walls of the chamber. They could then float it throughout the cavernous hall to be recognized by the Senate chair or to debate the issue at hand. In the center of the Senate chamber was the Chancellor's podium. Seated in it would be the Supreme Chancellor and the Senate's Vice Chair, who would lead sessions of congress.
The podium could be lowered to an office used by the Chancellor below the chamber.

An underworld of crime, poverty, and darkness existed beneath the upper levels of Nexara City-world.
Beneath the top-level structures, streets and glistening lights of the city surface was Nexara's underworld. Its underworld was known for its crime and smugglers. It was accessible by huge portals that additionally served as ventilation shafts. The underworld was a massive city beneath a city with hundreds of
levels
built on top of each other. On each level, dwellings and smaller buildings were squeezed in between massive towers and infrastructure that serviced the upper levels. The streets and alleyways were narrow and large sections contained massive buildings and warehouses, largely abandoned. Sunlight almost never reached the lower levels, which had to be artificially lit.
The population was able to move from one area to another via
trains
that ran below and above the planet's surface. While airspeeders were used, they were a less common sight than on the surface levels. The underworld, containing a vast population of the poor and criminal, was dark and violent. Those who wanted to disappear to hide from the authorities or to conduct illegal transactions could blend in despite the presence of cam automatons and other monitoring technology.
Patrolled by the underworld police division of the Nexara City-world Security Flux, the underworld was where large populations of species not loyal to the Overlord were relocated to after the formation of the Dominion. Uprisings and riots became common, with legion vanguard frequently called in to assist the Nexara City-world cops in putting down any dissent.
Level 2046
Level 2046 was one of thousands of levels that made up the ecumenopolis of Nexara City-world.
Level 2046 was a middle level in the city. Home to a thriving community of sellers and industrialists, the sector housed dozens of shops and even a meat packing plant. Yet, there was also many impoverished citizens and desperate people who lived on this level. By the Dominion Era, the economy of the area had started to decline, even with the Dominion's ruthless crackdown on crime. The increased oppression and protection racketeering from troopers hurt many of the businesses there, and corrupt politicians, such as Senator Daho Sejan, would use the level as a way of increasing the financial power. This level also featured at least one
Desi's Noodles
, a common food chain of popular fast food stores that were located in the Nexara City-world Underworld. Eating there was considered a must for travelers to the planet. Many legion vanguard frequented the locations for their famous dishes.

Nexara's entertainment districts catered to everyone from the wealthy and powerful to the secretive and criminal. The upper sections of the Uscru District reached to upper levels of the city where the
Galaxies Opera House
was located. It was here that Chancellor Draven Nul often would come to enjoy various performances. He also met Xarn Velgor and told him the story of
Darth Plagueis
to subvert Kast's faith in the Keepers.

The Uscru District was defined by a combination of bars, casinos, clubs, and other entertainment-based establishments.
Further down in the lower levels of the Uscru Entertainment District was a variety of establishments, most notably the
Outlander Club
. The club was situated in a less developed area of the district and did not garner as much attention from the police, a fact that worked in the club's favor. Its labyrinth of bars, gambling rooms and sleeping quarters allowed for a wide variety of visitors and entertainment. In the center was a large bar, usually crowded, that catered to all parts of Nexara's society.
Keeper Knights Malkor and Kast pursued bounty hunter
Zam Wesell
into the Uscru District after her attempted assassination of Senator Amidala. The pursuit ended in the Outlander Club, where the Keepers located the assassin. However, Wesell was killed by bounty hunter Jango Fett in an alley outside the club before the Keepers could finish questioning her.

Nexara's industrial zone, known as The Works, was once a manufacturing powerhouse known throughout the stellar realm.
A large sector and industrial area,
The Works
was once a great manufacturing district, recognized as a major source of production for
spacecraft
parts,
construction automatons
, and building materials. However, centuries prior to the Gene Wars, increasingly high cost on Nexara City-world prompted most manufacturers to shift their operations to off-planet facilities. Afterwards, the Works mainly consisted of long-abandoned buildings and empty warehouses, many of which were appropriated by criminals, with large areas rendered uninhabitable by toxic waste. Because of this, a derelict hangar in the neglected industrial zone was the site of clandestine meetings between Draven Nul and Darth Tyranus during the Gene Wars.
