# Veldara planet

The Forest Moon of Veldara circles a gas giant in a system near the galactic frontier. ―The Galactic Explorer's Guide Veldara , also called Tana by the Moorlings native to one of its , was a gas giant located in the Veldara system binary star system positioned in the Moddell sector of the stellar realm 's Outer Rim Territories . Veldara acted almost like a third sun to the moons with its clouds of hydrogen reflecting light.
Located in grid square H-16 on the Standard Galactic Grid , it was connected to Cerea and Bakura by a voidspace route The planet was orbited by nine major moons, the largest of which was known as the Forest Moon of Veldara . The ocean moon of Kef Bir was also one of Veldara's moons. Additionally, 48 "sub-lunar" objects orbited the planet.
Before the destruction of the New Concordium , the Galactic Senate attempted to rename the planet Tana, in honor of the Moorlings.
