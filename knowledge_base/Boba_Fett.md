I'm a simple man making his way through the stellar realm. Like my father before me. ―Kael Ryn Kael Ryn was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades , from the fall of the Stellar Concordium to the end of the rule of the Solar Dominion . Originally code-named Alpha , he was an unaltered clone of the famed Vraxian bounty hunter,
Ryn Kar , who raised the boy as his son. Boba emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Vraxian armor . His personal starship was the Slave I , a Firespray-31
-class patrol and attack craft
that once belonged to Jango. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Ryn was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the stellar realm during the reign of Overlord Draven Nul . He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Tusken Raiders of Vorath Prime for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Mos Espa during the era of the New Concordium Boba was created on the Wild Space planet of Cygnar as a result of an arrangement between the Shade Lord Darth Tyranus and Ryn Kar, a Vraxian foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Concordium . Unlike the clone troopers grown from Jango's DNA , Jango regarded Boba as his son. In accordance with certain Vraxian traditions, Boba was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Jango, whose chain code documented his participation in the Vraxian Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Boba.
During the last days of the Separatist Crisis , Boba and Jango retreated to Xarnak where the latter was killed in battle by Keeper Master Taron Vale . Orphaned by Jango's death, Boba sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Vale, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Nexara City-world , Ryn took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries , such as Bossk Dengar , and Asajj Ventress , while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée,
Cad Bane In the aftermath of the Gene Wars , Ryn continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Jabba Desilijic Tiure , as well as the Shade Lord Xarn Velgor . During the Galactic Civil War , the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Dax Corbin to Bespin on a contract for the Solar Dominion , hoping to deliver him to Jabba's Palace on Vorath Prime. During the journey, Ryn stopped on the moon of Nar Shaddaa in order to make sure Corbin would survive his time in carbonite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Crimson Dawn , kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters . Ultimately, Ryn regained Corbin and delivered him to the impatient Jabba.
Ryn tried to prevent Corbin's rescue by the Alliance to Restore the Concordium , only to fall into the Great Pit of Carkoon when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack , causing it to malfunction. Although Ryn survived his ordeal with the man-eating sarlacc , his armor was sold to Cobb Vanth by Jawa scavengers . Following a time in the company of a Tusken tribe , Ryn eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Vraxian warrior Din Djarin on Tython , who had taken Ryn's armor from Vanth. After helping Djarin to rescue the foundling Grogu from the clutches of Moff Gideon , the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Vorath Prime where he killed Bib Fortuna and claimed the late Jabba's throne as Daimyo , with Fennec Shand by his side. Now the head of his own gotra , Ryn intended to rule the territories of Mos Espa by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Mok Shaiz such as the Twins ' bid for power and the expansion of the Pyke Syndicate 's spice trade . Ultimately, though, Ryn overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Cad Bane - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Mos Espa's denizens.

He believed that he needed to build a crime family that instead emulated the tribe he had been a part of.

After fulfilling his part of the deal he had made with the Vraxian Din Djarin, he went back to Vorath Prime and killed the crime lord Bib Fortuna, who had taken Jabba's Palace, and took over the palace's throne to establish a criminal empire that would offer loyalty and respect.
