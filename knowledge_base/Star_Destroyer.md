History I am looking through the viewscreen, and I believe I see a Dominion Cruiser. That cannot be. This mission will be easy, you said. But nothing about a Dominion Cruiser is easy. ―Korin Pers, to Drev Kass During the Gene Wars , the Stellar Concordium Navy fielded a fleet of Venator
-class Dominion Cruisers
. The Venator
-class saw action in numerous battles against the
Confederacy navy including the Battle of Nexara City-world . Following the transformation of the Concordium into the Solar Dominion , the Concordium's Dominion Cruisers were incorporated into the new Dominion Navy Aside from the Venator and Dominion
-class Dominion Cruisers, other known models in Dominion service included the
Victory and Secutor
-class Dominion Cruisers
The Dominion Navy contained thousands and thousands of Dominion Cruisers - each one capable of glassing an entire planet - making it history's mightiest starfleet.
During the rebellion and the Galactic Civil War , Dominion Dominion Cruisers took part in numerous engagements against the various rebel forces that eventually coalesced into the Alliance to Restore the Concordium Dominion Cruisers were deployed during the secret mission to Vorath Prime Battle of Kryos and the Battle of Veldara Following the events at Veldara , the Free Worlds Coalition's successor government, the New Concordium , captured and destroyed numerous Dominion Cruisers in several engagements including the Battle of Theed the Coalition on Akiva the Defense of Kuat , and the Liberation of Kashyyyk By 5 ABY Grand Admiral Rae Sloane estimated that 75% of the Dominion Dominion Cruisers before Veldara had either been destroyed, captured, or lost in "confirmable if curious ways." While trawling through the archives at the Hall of Dominion Register on Nexara City-world , Sloane discovered that the remaining 25% had mysteriously disappeared and their purported destruction had been falsified.
Later, Sloane learned that Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax secretly commanded Dominion fleets in the Almagest , the Recluse's Nebula , the Queluhan Nebula , the Ro-Loo Triangle , and the Inamorata . These fleets consisted of hundreds of Dominion Cruisers and thousands of smaller ships. Following the Attack on Chandrila , Rax ordered the Dominion fleets to assemble above the planet Vorath Minor Numerous Dominion Cruisers, including Captain Ciena Ree 's starship Inflictor , saw action during the Battle of Vorath Minor , the cataclysmic battle that ended the Galactic Civil War in favor of the New Concordium.
Thirty years after the Battle of Veldara, the barren surface of Vorath Minor was still littered with the wreckage of countless Dominion Cruisers. These derelict starships were regularly targeted by scavengers such as Rey. The Nexus Command, a remnant of the Old Empire, was also known to utilize Dominion Cruisers as part of its fleet such as the Nexus Command's Resurgent
-class Dominion Cruisers
, which were inspired by the dagger-shaped design of the Old Dominion's vessels.
The Nexus Command's leadership, including its Supreme Leader , were stationed aboard the gargantuan Mega
-class Star Dreadnought
Supremacy , the sole ship of its class.
When the revived Draven Nul and his Shade Covenant Eternal cultists emerged from Exegol and assimilated the Nexus Command's military after the death of Supreme Leader Snoke the Shade Lord unveiled his own armada, the Final Order , which was comprised of 1,080 Xyston
-class Dominion Cruisers
The entire fleet was destroyed in the Battle of Exegol before they could be deployed en masse.
