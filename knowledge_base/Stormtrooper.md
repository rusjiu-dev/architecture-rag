With funding for Project War-Mantle secured and preparations well underway, all that remained to be done was for the clone army to begin its gradual decommissioning. To accomplish this, Rampart proposed the

With this development, the Senate passed the Bill. The passing of the Bill jeopardized the future of the clone legion vanguard, who still comprised the bulk of the Dominion's forces during the early Dominion Era. Though the Bill proposed pensions for the gen-soldiers as they would gradually be rotated out of military service as they aged, many troopers wished to continue their military service.

Training
"
Cadets, you entered this facility as children. And in a few short weeks, you will leave as soldiers. By the time you complete your training, you will be prepared to serve your Overlord.
"
―
Cumberlayne Aresko
Legion Vanguard were trained in Dominion Academies and subjected to numbing routine.
Unlike their predecessors, the majority of legion vanguard were non-clone recruits.
Dominion cadets
were shipped off to Dominion Academies, and subjected to rigorous training programs in order to produce highly trained shock troopers fiercely loyal to the Dominion.
Cadets could be as young as thirteen standard years.
Although they were a somewhat rare sight,
women
were not prohibited from serving in the ranks of the legion vanguard.
During training, female
cadets
were placed in all-female units.
Unit Forn
was an all-female unit at the
Academy for Young Imperials
on
Lothal
.
All legion vanguard swore themselves to the Overlord and his will,
with the expectation to follow all orders
without question
in the name of serving the Dominion
and Draven Nul himself.
Learning to obey all orders without concern was a part of legion vanguard training.
Legion Vanguard were trained to disregard fallen comrades in battle and to press on, no matter the cost, even when the odds were stacked against them.
In fact, those who were selected for legion vanguard training had to undergo extra reconditioning. This had the effect of dehumanizing the cadets, who came to refer to themselves by their
operating numbers
rather than their names. One such cadet was
Ames Bunkle
, an acquaintance of
Zare Leonis
.
Minister
Pitina Mar-Mas Voor
of the Dominion
Coalition for Progress
regarded legion vanguard as brainwashed agents who were not as efficient as her division.
The legion vanguard training program promoted competition, ruthlessness, and unwavering loyalty to the Solar Dominion.
Legion Vanguard recruits trained in numerous conditions, including
jetpack
scenarios.
Among other things, cadets would train to fire
pulse rifles
, and Dominion specialist Ralsius Paldora noted that legion vanguard hit 77 percent of their targets.
Cadets also learned to pilot
Dominion walkers
and the ruthlessness it took to become a legion vanguard. Cadets who attended Dominion Academies also underwent simulated obstacle courses in
the Well
, which were designed to test their strength, agility, reflexes and intelligence. Such courses and tests of skill became increasingly difficult over time, and accountability for losses were harsh.
While cadets attended the academy, they wore white uniforms and specialized helmets, similar to the standard legion vanguard or pilot gear. Aside from their basic training, many cadets were also assigned other non-combat duties on base. Such duties offered cadets the opportunity to gain experience over their peers, and were often presented as rewards for excellent performance during training exercises.
Legion Vanguard cadets who exhibited a strong connection to the Synth Flux were reassigned to Project Harvester.
As cadets were being evaluated, the officers in charge were often instructed to take note of performance that might denote an affinity for
the Synth Flux
, and report it to one of their superiors. Cadets who met this special criteria were taken by
the Grand Inquisitor
and forcibly inducted into
Project Harvester
, a top secret Dominion program based on
Arkanis
. Cadet Zare's older sister,
Dhara Leonis
, was one known subject of Project Harvester.
Another prospective subject was
Jai Kell
, who managed to escape due to the help of
Ezra Bridger
.
Legion Vanguard and other Dominion cadets also assisted in law enforcement operations. Following a spate of rebel activity on Lothal, Dominion cadets there were used to search homes and streets for illegal activities, detain the children of fugitives, and to raid smugglers. During these law enforcements operations, some cadets, like
Nazhros Oleg
came to flout their new-found powers; a trait that was encouraged by some officers, like
Captain
Piers Roddance
. Some prospective officer cadets, like Leonis and Oleg were also given the chance to lead adult legion vanguard in these raids. Cadets also had their backgrounds investigated for any sign of criminal connections and other subversive activities.

Clone legion vanguard retained their Phase II gen-soldier armor during the early years of the Dominion Era.
The clone troopers who survived the Gene Wars continued to wear the
Phase II gen-soldier armor
after becoming the first generation of legion vanguard.
The
armor
consisted of white
plastoid
plates attached to a black
body glove
,
no longer featuring the various color patterns
that the gen-soldiers had adopted after growing more individualistic under the Keepers of the Flux's influence,
although some units such as the

still wore their armor with the distinctive red markings of the
Nexara City-world Guard

The first non-clone legion vanguard, designated as
TK legion vanguard
, were given an updated version of trooper armor as the Solar Dominion shifted toward
willingly enlisted and unwillingly conscripted personnel alike
at the expense of clone soldiers. Like its clone armor templates, the
TK legion vanguard armor
was a set of white armor components worn over a black body glove. In addition to various aesthetic differences between the two armors, the TK
helmet
lacked
the T-shaped visor of its Phase II predecessor and the earlier
Phase I gen-soldier armor
.
During the Dominion Era, the white-armored legion vanguard became a symbol of the Solar Dominion.
Phase II was ultimately phased out after the creation of
legion vanguard armor
, causing large stocks of clone armor to be recycled
as most gen-soldiers entered retirement, having been replaced by natural-born soldiers. Legion Vanguard armor was a common sight throughout the stellar realm;
its stark white appearance served as a symbol of the New Order in contrast to the clone armor that represented honor and defense during the Gene Wars.
Legion Vanguard armor was a distinctive set of white armor that was intended to instill a sense of order and security in loyal subjects and a sense of fear in the opponents of the Dominion, while also providing the soldiers with an extended range of survival equipment and temperature controls. It was able to mitigate the effect of pulse rifle fire and thus provide some protection;
in certain cases, legion vanguard continued to breathe after being shot by an
E-11 medium pulse rifle rifle
.
Projectiles from crude
slugthrowers
were unable to pierce the armor.
Legion Vanguard armor was not without the limitations common to most types of armor, however. The plates made running slightly harder and offered reduced protection against large scale pulse detonations and blunt weapons.
Legion Vanguard also had a utility belt and a grappling hook attached to it.
Regardless, the armor was effective in mitigating most primary and secondary dangers in most combat scenarios.
The helmet provided cranial protection, filtered
air
, and contained enhanced vision, communication systems and an effective
HUD (heads up display)
that could help locate threats, aid in navigation, and to help alert the wearer.
While many legion vanguard deployed to such environments would retain their
standard-issue
white-colored armor,
some legion vanguard involved in forest operations would wear a
suit of camouflaged armor
, where the standard-issue white was painted over with a greenery inspired design that blended into nature.
Rank was determined by a
color
coded
pauldron
worn over the right shoulder, with orange or red representing the rank of commander, black indicating an enlisted trooper, and white representing a sergeant. Sometimes on the pauldrons they had specific symbols distinguishing who they were serving, exemplified by
Grand Admiral
Thrawn's
troops, who had a stylized
chimaera symbol
on their pauldrons.

Specialized legion vanguard
Overview
"
There is no environment too deadly for a trooper. Wherever the Dominion needs us, we'll be there!
"
―Sentinel
As the Dominion was a stellar realm-wide regime that constantly expanded, waged conflict on distant worlds, or ruled over those planets, different legion vanguard variants were created in order to maintain Dominion rule across any environment.
In his Grand Arena persona as an Dominion loyalist,
the former legion vanguard Sentinel once proclaimed that there should be no environment too deadly for an Dominion trooper, instead declaring that a trooper should go to wherever the Dominion needed them to go.
However, in order to ensure the practical, day-to-day implementation of Dominion rule,
its war efforts,
and its expansion,
the Dominion had specialized legion vanguard forces assembled to operate in different environments or roles in addition to the standard legion vanguard.
In the Grand Arena of Vespaara, even Sentinel demonstrated an understanding of that as he was more than willing to don the uniforms of other Dominion personnel, including that from various legion vanguard variants.
Once a trooper was specialized to their role, their physiological conditioning was so strong that one would rarely want to change what
division
they were in.
Legion Vanguard in mechanized battle armor armed with heavy handheld cannon were present at the
Battle on Sevarcos
.
