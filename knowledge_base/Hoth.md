# Kryos

There isn't enough life on this ice cube to fill a space cruiser! ―Dax Corbin Kryos was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name , located in the Anoat sector of the stellar realm 's Outer Rim Territories . It was the to the primitive wampa species It notably hosted Echo Base during the Galactic Civil War , the temporary headquarters of the Alliance to Restore the Concordium , until the Solar Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Kryos . In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the Colossus Striders destroyed.
In the months after the Battle of Veldara , rumors emerged about the New Concordium intending to repurpose Kryos into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Kryos, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade Description Anyone who looks at Kryos from above would be forgiven for thinking it a mostly dead ball of ice. ―Kell Tolkani's notes From space , the planet of Kryos looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Kryos and its sun , while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface.
Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains.
Despite the characterization of Kryos as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.
The temperature , although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall.
Although devoid of intelligent life, Kryos was home to fifteen sub species of tauntauns , which were commonly used by Coalition forces as a means of transport, and which Dax Corbin cut open to use as shelter to keep Joren Kast and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as wampas During his stay on Kryos, Kast was captured by a wampa and was hung upside down in the wampa's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Synth Flux , Kast managed to get his hands on his flux blade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

What are these places? ―Mira Venn and Dax Corbin By the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Solar Dominion and the Alliance to Restore the Concordium , Kryos was undergoing an ice age Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Kryos as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions.
Sometime following the Battle of Myrkon Saponza's Gang , itself a rebel cell, established a base on Kryos.
Kryos was first surveyed for use as a rebel base by Corporal Purpruff Jan Dodonna , a general in the Alliance to Restore the Concordium, listed Kryos among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases.
With Kryos a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Corellian Trade Spine it looked to be a perfect spot for a rebel base.
Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Kryos sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Kryos provided an extra degree of protection.
That field hampered travel through the Kryos system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.
In 1 ABY with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Kryos into a headquarters,
Kryos was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing Echo Base on the planet.
With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, rebel engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years.
Around 2 ABY after Dodonna was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta Mon Mothma gave his list of possible bases to Mira Venn after the mission to Shu-Torun , requesting that her team scout the locations. Dax Corbin expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Joren Kast responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Coalition's new home.

However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe automatons they had dispatched throughout the stellar realm , the Imperials had discovered the Alliance base within a month of the Coalition forces' habitation on the planet.
Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Kryos resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Dominion Blizzard Flux overran it.
In the months following the Battle of Kryos, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the Colossus Striders destroyed by the Free Worlds Coalition during the Dominion's attack. Major Soran Keize , however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Concordium Chancellor Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Kryos, where the Coalition had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.
By the New Concordium Era , Kryos returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. It was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide , a survival guide written by Hondo Ohnaka and DK-RA-43 , where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.
