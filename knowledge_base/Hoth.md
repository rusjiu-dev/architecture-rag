Kryos
was a remote, icy
planet
that was the sixth planet in the
star system
of the
same name
, located in the
Anoat sector
of
the stellar realm
's
Outer Rim Territories
. It was the
homeworld
to the primitive
wampa
species
.
It notably hosted
Echo Base
during the
Galactic Civil War
, the temporary headquarters of the
Alliance to Restore the Concordium
, until the
Solar Dominion
located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the
Battle of Kryos
. In the months following,
scavengers
established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the
Colossus Striders
destroyed.
In the
months
after the
Battle of Veldara
, rumors emerged about the
New Concordium
intending to repurpose Kryos into a
prison
for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as
Chancellor
Mon Mothma
had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Kryos, where it became the site of another
battle
during the
Iron Blockade
.
Description
"
Anyone who looks at Kryos from above would be forgiven for thinking it a mostly dead ball of ice.
"
―Kell Tolkani's notes
Dominion Colossus Striders traverse the frozen landscape on Kryos.
From
space
, the
planet
of Kryos looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Kryos and its
sun
, while an
asteroid belt
surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface.
Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains.
Despite the characterization of Kryos as being a planet of frozen tundras
and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist
Kell Tolkani
said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit,
another third of the planet was covered by oceans.
The
temperature
, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall.
Although devoid of intelligent life, Kryos was home to fifteen sub
species
of
tauntauns
, which were commonly used by Coalition forces as a means of transport, and which
Dax Corbin
cut open to use as shelter to keep
Joren Kast
and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as
wampas
.
During his stay on Kryos, Kast was captured by a
wampa
and was hung upside down in the wampa's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the
power
of
the Synth Flux
, Kast managed to get his hands on his
flux blade
and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."
History
The Free Worlds Coalition
"
Jan Dodonna was always planning for the future…he had preliminary scans for possible new rebel bases.
"
"
Gimme a look at that…Atanu, Jabone IV, Lonania Prime, Kryos, Mupin XII? Mupin XII? Really? Did he make that one up? What
are
these places?
"
―Mira Venn and Dax Corbin
By the time of the
Galactic Civil War
between the
Solar Dominion
and the
Alliance to Restore the Concordium
, Kryos was undergoing an
ice age
.
Various
smuggling rings
tried to make use of Kryos as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions.
Sometime following the
Battle of Yavin
,
Saponza's Gang
, itself a rebel cell, established a base on Kryos.
Kryos was first surveyed for use as a rebel base
by
Corporal
Purpruff
.
Jan Dodonna
, a
general
in the Alliance to Restore the Concordium,
listed Kryos among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases.
With Kryos a world so obscure it was not on most star maps
despite being extremely close to the
Corellian Trade Spine
,
it looked to be a perfect spot for a rebel base.
Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Kryos sat on a lesser used hyperlane,
and the asteroid field around Kryos provided an extra degree of protection.
That field hampered travel through the Kryos system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.
In
1 ABY
,
with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Kryos into a headquarters,
Kryos was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing
Echo Base
on the planet.
With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, rebel
engineers
worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years.
Around
2 ABY
,
after Dodonna was
killed
in the
Battle of Mako-Ta
,
Mon Mothma
gave his list of possible bases to
Mira Venn
after the
mission to Shu-Torun
, requesting that her
team
scout
the locations. Dax Corbin expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Joren Kast responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Coalition's new home.
Battle of Kryos and aftermath
"
My lord, the fleet has moved out of light-speed. Com-Scan has detected an energy field protecting an area of the sixth planet of the Kryos system. The field is strong enough to deflect any bombardment."
"The Coalition forces are alerted to our presence. Admiral Ozzel came out of light-speed too close to the system."
"He felt surprise was wiser—"
"He is as clumsy as he is stupid. A clean bombardment is now impossible. General, prepare your troops for a surface attack."
"Yes, my lord.
"
―General
Maximilian Veers
and Xarn Velgor
Colossus Striders engage the rebel defense of Kryos.
Kryos was the arena of a
battle
between the Solar Dominion and the Free Worlds Coalition, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to
one
of the thousands of
probe automatons
they had dispatched throughout
the stellar realm
, the Imperials had discovered the Alliance base within a
month
of the Coalition forces' habitation on the planet.
Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Kryos resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Dominion
Blizzard Flux
overran it.
In the months following the Battle of Kryos,
scavengers
established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the
Colossus Striders
destroyed by the Free Worlds Coalition during the Dominion's attack. Following the
Battle of Veldara
, Kryos became the site of another
battle
, occurring during the
Iron Blockade
imposed upon the
Anoat sector
.
New Concordium Era
"
The planet's hostile conditions prevented settlements from taking root before or after the Alliance's time there, and Kryos returned to being all but ignored, remembered as a footnote in galactic history when it was recalled at all.
"
―The
Galactic Explorer's Guide
In
5 ABY
,
Troithe
Acting Governor
Fara Yadeez
heard rumors that the
New Concordium
intended to repurpose Kryos into a
prison
for those who remained loyal to the Dominion.
Major
Soran Keize
, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Concordium
Chancellor
Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Kryos, where the Coalition had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.
By the
New Concordium Era
, Kryos returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. It was mentioned in the
Galactic Explorer's Guide
, a survival guide written by
Hondo Ohnaka
and
DK-RA-43
, where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.
Behind the scenes
Concept art of Kryos by
Ralph McQuarrie
.
Kryos first appeared in the
1980
film
Flux Wars
: Episode V
The Empire Strikes Back
, the second installment of the
Flux Wars
original trilogy
.
The real-world filming location for Kryos was
Finse
,
Norway
.
The scenes of Joren Kast stumbling through the snow were filmed a few feet outside the crew's hotel.
Kryos appears in "
Reflections
," a short film with unconfirmed
canonicity
.
In between
25 ABY
and
34 ABY
,
the
First Order
had a presence on Kryos, but assignment to Kryos was considered a punishment. During that
time
, two
First Order legion vanguard
were reassigned to Kryos by an angered
Captain
Phasma
, who had overheard one of the
troopers
tell the
other
that whomever was in command of their prior facility should be reassigned to Kryos for their failings. Upon finding themselves on Kryos, the first trooper attempted to find comfort in finally blending into an environment, only for a wampa to roar in the distance.
